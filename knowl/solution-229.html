Explanation 2.5.3.42.1.
\(\begin{aligned} B \amp = p-\frac{8b}{t} \\ t \cdot B \amp = t \cdot\left( p- t \frac{8b}{t}\right) \\ t \cdot B \amp = t \cdot p - t \cdot \frac{8b}{t} \\ tB \amp = tp -8b \\ tB \mathbf{{} -tp}\amp = tp -8b\mathbf{{} -tp} \\ tB-tp \amp = -8b \\ \frac{tB-tp}{-8} \amp = \frac{-8b}{-8} \\ {-\frac{tB-tp}{8}} \amp = b\\ b \amp = {-\frac{tB-tp}{8}} \end{aligned}\)
Note that you can rewrite the solution in a different format:
\(\begin{aligned} -\frac{tB-tp}{8}\amp =\frac{(-1)(tB-tp)}{8}\\ \amp =\frac{(-1) \cdot tB + (-1) \cdot (-tp)}{8}\\ \amp = \frac{-tB+tp}{8}\\ \amp = \frac{tp-tB}{8} \end{aligned}\)
