Exercise 5.
Consider the equation
\(y=\frac{4}{5} x-5\)
Which of the following ordered pairs are solutions to the given equation? There may be more than one correct answer.
  • \(\displaystyle (-5,-8)\)
  • \(\displaystyle (5,-1)\)
  • \(\displaystyle (-25,-25)\)
  • \(\displaystyle (0,0)\)
