1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{r}{9} \amp = 4 \\ 9 \cdot \frac{r}{9} \amp = 9 \cdot 4 \\ r \amp = 36 \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \(36\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(36\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{t}{c} \amp =\; n \\ c \cdot \frac{t}{c} \amp =\; c \cdot n \\ t \amp =\; {cn} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({cn}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({cn}\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
