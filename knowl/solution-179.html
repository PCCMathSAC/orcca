Explanation 2.4.4.36.1.
\(\begin{aligned} 2(k-10) \amp \leq 2(k-4) \\ 2k-2 \cdot 10 \amp \leq 2k-2 \cdot 4 \\ 2k-20 \amp \leq 2k-8 \\ 2k-20\mathbf{{} -2k+20} \amp \leq 2k-8\mathbf{{} -2k+20} \\ 0 \amp \leq 12 \end{aligned}\)
Since \(0 \leq 12\) is true, the solution set for this inequality is all real numbers.
