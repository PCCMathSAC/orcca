To be an rational number, you either have to be a fraction with whole numbers for the numerator and denominator, or you have to be a number with a terminating decimal expression, or you have to be a number with a repeating decimal expression. (Actually, these descriptions are a little redundant.) Sometimes rational numbers are explicitly written in one of these ways, and sometimes they are hidden. For example \(\sqrt{0.16}\) is a rational number, since \(\sqrt{0.16}=0.4\text{.}\) So the correct answers are BDEFGH.
