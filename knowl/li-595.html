Item 3.5.7.70.c.
In one month, you had a cell phone bill of \({\$34.70}\text{.}\) To find how many minutes you spent on the phone that month, we substitute \(34.7\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.045x+14\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.045x+14 \\ 34.7 \amp = 0.045x +14 \\ 34.7\mathbf{{}-14} \amp = 0.045x +14\mathbf{{}-14}\\ 20.7 \amp = 0.045x \\ \frac{20.7}{0.045} \amp = \frac{0.045x}{0.045} \\ 460 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
If your bill was \({\$34.70}\) in one month, you must have spent \(460\) minutes on the phone.
