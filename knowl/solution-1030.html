Explanation A.4.3.25.1.

Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(592\) is \(x\%\) of \(740\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(592\) out of \(740\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{592}{740} \\ 740x \amp = 592 \cdot 100 \\ 740x \amp = 59200 \\ \frac{740x}{740} \amp = \frac{59200}{740} \\ x \amp = 80 \end{aligned} }\)
\(592\) is \(80\%\) of \(740\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(592\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(740\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 592 \amp = x \cdot 740 \\ \frac{592}{740} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 740}{740} \\ 0.8 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 80\% \end{aligned} }\)
\(592\) is \(80\%\) of \(740\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “\(592\) is what percent of \(740\text{,}\)”
  • \(592\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(740\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{592}{740} = 0.8 = 80\% }\)
\(592\) is \(80\%\) of \(740\text{.}\)
