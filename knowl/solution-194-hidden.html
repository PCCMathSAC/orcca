Two approaches are shown below.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 10 \amp = -5(A -4) \amp 10 \amp = -5(A -4)\\ 10 \amp = -5(A +(-4)) \amp \frac{10}{-5} \amp = \frac{-5(A -4)}{-5}\\ 10 \amp = -5A +(-5) \cdot (-4) \amp -2 \amp = A -4\\ 10 \amp = -5A +20 \amp -2\mathbf{{}+4} \amp = A -4\mathbf{{}+4}\\ 10 \mathbf{{}-20} \amp = -5A +20\mathbf{{}-20} \amp 2 \amp = A\\ -10 \amp = -5A \amp A \amp = 2\\ \frac{-10}{-5} \amp = \frac{-5A}{-5} \\ 2 \amp = A\\ A \amp = 2 \end{aligned} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(2\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(A\text{,}\) some report \(A = 2\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{2\}\text{,}\) or that \(A\in\left\{2\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(2\) in for \(A\) in the original equation \(10=-5\mathopen{}\left(A-4\right)\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
