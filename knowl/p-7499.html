We can see Tree 1’s equation is \(y=\frac{2}{3}x+2\text{,}\) and Tree 2’s equation is \(y=\frac{2}{3}x+5\text{.}\) Tree 1 was \(2\) feet tall when it was planted, and Tree 2 was \(5\) feet tall when it was planted.
