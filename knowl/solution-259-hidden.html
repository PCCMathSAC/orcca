We substitute in each ordered pair’s \(x\)- and \(y\)-values into the equation \(y=10 x+9\text{,}\) and see whether the resulting equation is true.
In two cases, \((-2,-11)\) and \((-4,-31)\text{,}\) we have true equations:
\(\begin{aligned} -11 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 10(-2)+9 \amp -31 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 10(-4)+9\\ -11 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} -20+9 \amp -31 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} -40+9\\ -11 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} -11 \amp -31 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} -31 \end{aligned}\)
So \((-2,-11)\) and \((-4,-31)\) are solutions to the given equation.
In the other two cases, \((8,90)\) and \((0,12)\text{,}\) we have false equations:
\(\begin{aligned} 90 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 10(8)+9 \amp 12 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 10(0)+9\\ 90 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 80+9 \amp 12 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 0+9\\ 90 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 89 \amp 12 \amp \stackrel{?}{=} 9 \end{aligned}\)
So \((8,90)\) and \((0,12)\) are not solutions.
