Explanation A.1.7.38.1.

When we add up two negative numbers, the result will be negative.
We need to find the sum of their absolute value. To add two decimals, make sure to line up the decimal points:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{+3}2.91 \\ \amp \underline{+3 1.8\phantom{ad}} \\ \amp \phantom{+}34.71 \end{aligned} }\)
Since the result is negative, we have \(-2.91 + (-31.8) = -34.71\text{.}\)
