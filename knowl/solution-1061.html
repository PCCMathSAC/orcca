Explanation A.4.3.56.1.

First, we find the total number of students who participated in this survey:
\(\displaystyle{ 88+172+107+170+60=597 }\)
Now, this problem can be boiled down to this question: \(88\) is what percent of \(597\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(88\) is \(x\%\) of \(597\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(88\) out of \(597\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{88}{597} \\ 597x \amp = 88 \cdot 100 \\ 597x \amp = 8800 \\ \frac{597x}{597} \amp = \frac{8800}{597} \\ x \amp \approx 14.7403685092127 \\ x \amp \approx 15\% \end{aligned} }\)
Approximately \(15\) of students ride Bus #1.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(88\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(597\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 88 \amp = x \cdot 597 \\ \frac{88}{597} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 597}{597} \\ 0.147403685092127 \amp \approx x \\ x \amp \approx 15\% \end{aligned} }\)
Approximately \(15\) of students ride Bus #1.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(88\) is what percent of \(597\text{,}\)”
  • \(88\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(597\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{88}{597} \approx 0.147403685092127 \approx 15\% }\)
Approximately \(15\) of students ride Bus #1.
