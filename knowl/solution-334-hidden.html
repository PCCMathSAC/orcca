There are at least two methods to reduce the fraction \(\displaystyle{ \frac{60}{18} }\text{.}\)
Method 1:We find a number that divides into both the numerator \(60\text{,}\) and the denominator \(18\text{.}\)
Check the first few prime numbers one by one: \(2, 3, 5, 7, \ldots\)
In this case, \(2\) divides into both the numerator and denominator of \(\displaystyle{ \frac{60}{18} }\text{.}\) We divide \(2\) into both numbers, and we have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}\frac{60}{18} \amp = \frac{60 \div 2}{18\div 2}\\ \amp = \frac{30}{9} \end{aligned}}\)
Next, check again whether any prime number divides into both the numerator and denominator. We need to keep trying until no prime numbers divide into both numbers. In this problem, \(3\) divides into both the numerator and denominator for \(\displaystyle{ \frac{30}{9} }\text{.}\) We divide \(3\) into both numbers, and we have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}\frac{30}{9} \amp = \frac{30 \div 3}{9\div 3}\\\amp = \frac{10}{3} \end{aligned}}\)
Finally, we have: \(\displaystyle{ \frac{60}{18} = \frac{10}{3} }\)
Method 2:
A second method to reduce fraction is to prime factor both the numerator and the denominator, and then cancel out factors in pairs: one from the numerator and one from the denominator.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{60}{18} \amp = \frac{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5}{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 3} \\ \amp =\frac{2 \cdot 5}{3}\\ \amp = \frac{10}{3} \end{aligned} }\)
