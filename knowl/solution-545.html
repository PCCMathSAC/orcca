Explanation 3.7.5.46.1.
Assume the engine has been running for \(x\) hours since its tank was refilled, and assume there are \(y\) gallons of gas left in the tank. A linear equation to model the amount of gas in the tank is:
\(\displaystyle{ y = -4.5x+225 }\)
Since the equation is in slope-intercept mode, we can see its \(y\)-intercept is \((0,225)\text{.}\)
The correct solution is: The y-intercept is (0,225). It implies the engine started with 225 gallons of gas in its tank.
