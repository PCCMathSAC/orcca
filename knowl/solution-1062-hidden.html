This problem can be boiled down to this question: \(151.96\) is \(0.58\%\) of what? We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(151.96\) is \(0.58\%\) of \(x\text{,}\) so “\(0.58\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(151.96\) out of \(x\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{0.58}{100} \amp = \frac{151.96}{x} \\ 0.58x \amp = 100 \cdot 151.96 \\ 0.58x \amp = 15196 \\ \frac{0.58x}{0.58} \amp = \frac{15196}{0.58} \\ x \amp = 26200 \end{aligned} }\)

Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: \(151.96\) is \(0.58\%\) of what? Assume \(151.96\) is \(0.58\%\) of \(x\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} 151.96 \amp = 0.0058 \cdot x \\ \frac{151.96}{0.0058} \amp = \frac{0.0058x}{0.0058} \\ 26200 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)

Method 3
In the sentence “\(151.96\) is \(0.58\%\) of what,”
  • \(151.96\) is the percentage,
  • \(0.58\%\) is the rate,
  • “what” is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{base} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{rate}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{base} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{rate}} = \frac{151.96}{0.58\%} = \frac{151.96}{0.0058} = 26200 }\)
Ashley invested \({\$26{,}200.00}\) in this mutual fund.
