We substitute in each ordered pair’s \(x\)- and \(y\)-values into the equation \(y=-\frac{5}{6} x-2\text{,}\) and see whether the resulting equation is true.
In two cases, \((-18,13)\) and \((0,-2)\text{,}\) we have true equations:
\(\begin{aligned} 13 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-\frac{5}{6}(-18)-2 \amp -2 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-\frac{5}{6}(0)-2\\ 13 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}15-2 \amp -2 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}0-2\\ 13 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}13 \amp -2 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-2\\ \end{aligned}\)
So \((-18,13)\) and \((0,-2)\) are solutions to the given equation.
In the other two cases, \((12,-7)\) and \((-24,20)\text{,}\) we have false equations:
\(\begin{aligned} 20 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-\frac{5}{6}(-24)-2 \amp -7 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-\frac{5}{6}(12)-2\\ 20 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}20-2 \amp -7 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-10-2\\ 20 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}18 \amp -7 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-12\\ \end{aligned}\)
So \((12,-7)\) and \((-24,20)\) are not solutions.
