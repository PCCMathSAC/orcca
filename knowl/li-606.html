Item 3.5.7.72.b.
After \(28\) months, we can find the height of the tree if we substitute \(28\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.26x+16.2\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.26x+16.2 \\ y \amp = 0.26 \cdot 28 +16.2 \\ y \amp = 7.28+16.2 \\ y \amp = 23.48 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(28\) months, the tree is \(23.48\) feet tall.
