In the year 2020, the population of Bulgaria was \(6.934\) million. And it was shrinking at a rate of about \(0.046\,\frac{\text{million}}{\text{year}}\text{.}\) At this rate, how many years from 2020 will it take for Bulgaria’s population to reach \(5\) million? Set up an equation where the solution is the number of years it will take.
