Explanation 1.2.5.73.1.
The perimeter is the result from adding the six sides together: \({\frac{1}{2}}T + {\frac{3}{5}}h + {\frac{2}{3}}T + {\frac{3}{4}}h + {\frac{1}{7}}Y + {\frac{1}{2}}Y\text{.}\) There are three \(Y\)-terms, two \(Y\)-terms, and two \(T\)-terms. We combine each pair of like terms and the perimeter is \({\frac{27}{20}h+\frac{9}{14}Y+\frac{7}{6}T}\text{.}\)
