Item 3.6.5.64.c.
In one month, you had a cell phone bill of \({\$33.75}\text{.}\) To find how many minutes you spent on the phone that month, we substitute \(33.75\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.035(x-240)+26.4\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.035(x-240) +26.4 \\ 33.75 \amp = 0.035(x-240) +26.4 \\ 7.35 \amp = 0.035(x-240)\\ \frac{7.35}{0.035} \amp = x - 240\\ 210 \amp = x - 240\\ 450 \amp = x\\ 450 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
If your bill was \({\$33.75}\) in one month, you must have spent \(450\) minutes on the phone.
