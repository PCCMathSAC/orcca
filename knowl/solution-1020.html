Explanation A.4.3.15.1.

Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(30\%\) of \(780\) is \(x\text{,}\) so “\(30\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(x\) out of \(780\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{30}{100} \amp = \frac{x}{780} \\ 100x \amp = 30 \cdot 780 \\ 100x \amp = 23400 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{23400}{100} \\ x \amp = 234 \end{aligned} }\)
\(30\%\) of \(780\) is \(234\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: What is \(30\%\) of \(780\text{?}\) Assume \(x\) is \(30\%\) of \(780\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} x \amp = 0.3 \cdot 780 \\ \amp = 234 \end{aligned} }\)
\(30\%\) of \(780\) is \(234\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “\(30\%\) of \(780\) is what,”
  • “what” is the percentage,
  • \(30\%\) is the rate,
  • \(780\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{percentage} = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base}\text{,}\) we do a multiplication to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{percentage } = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base} = 30\% \cdot 780 = 0.3 \cdot 780 = 234 }\)
\(30\%\) of \(780\) is \(234\text{.}\)
