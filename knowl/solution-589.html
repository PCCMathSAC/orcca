Explanation 3.8.5.39.1.
Since \(y=9\) is horizontal, the unknown line must also be horizontal (because they are parallel). Assume its equation is \(y=b\text{.}\)
Since the unknown line passes through \((-1,-10)\text{,}\) \(b\)’s value must be \(-10\text{.}\) The unknown line’s equation is \({y = -10}\)
