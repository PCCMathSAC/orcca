After substituting \(y\) in the equation with \(1800\text{,}\) we will solve for \(x\text{,}\) and find the answer we seek.
\begin{align*} y\amp=-130(x-2013)+2975\\ \substitute{1800}\amp=-130(x-2013)+2975\\ 1800\subtractright{2975}\amp=-130(x-2013)\\ -1175\amp=-130(x-2013)\\ \divideunder{-1175}{-130}\amp=\divideunder{-130(x-2013)}{-130}\\ 9.038\amp\approx x-2013\\ 9.038\addright{2013}\amp\approx x\\ 2022\amp\approx x \end{align*}
We find that at this rate the company is headed toward a collapse in 2022.
