Item 2.5.3.12.b.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] r - b \amp = 9 \\ r - b\mathbf{{} +b} \amp = 9\mathbf{{} +b} \\ r \amp = {9+b} \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({9+b}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({9+b}\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
