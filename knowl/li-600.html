Item 3.5.7.71.b.
After \(36\) minutes, we can find the mass of the remaining gas if we substitute \(36\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-9.9x+465.3\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -9.9x+465.3 \\ y \amp = -9.9 \cdot 36 +465.3 \\ y \amp = -356.4+465.3 \\ y \amp = 108.9 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(36\) minutes, there are \(108.9\) grams of gas remaining.
