  1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] y+t \amp = q \\ y+t\mathbf{{}-t} \amp = q\mathbf{{}-t} \\ y \amp = {q-t} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({q-t}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({q-t}\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
