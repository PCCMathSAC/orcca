Sometimes the variable is in the numerator of a fraction, like in \(\frac{3x}{4}\text{.}\) This is actually the same as \(\frac{3}{4}x\text{.}\) Either way, \(x\) is multipled by \(3\) and divided by \(4\text{.}\) So this is another situation where the reciprocal of a fraction (in this case the reciproocal of \(\frac{3}{4}\)) can help.
