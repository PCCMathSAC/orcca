Item 3.6.5.66.b.
After \(29\) months, we can find the height of the tree if we substitute \(29\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.23(x-11)+14.23\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.23(29-11) +14.23 \\ y \amp = 18.37 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(29\) months, the tree is \(18.37\) feet tall.
