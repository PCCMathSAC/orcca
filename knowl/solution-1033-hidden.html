Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(98\) is \(x\%\) of \(70\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(98\) out of \(70\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{98}{70} \\ 70x \amp = 98 \cdot 100 \\ 70x \amp = 9800 \\ \frac{70x}{70} \amp = \frac{9800}{70} \\ x \amp = 140 \end{aligned} }\)
\(98\) is \(140\%\) of \(70\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(98\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(70\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 98 \amp = x \cdot 70 \\ \frac{98}{70} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 70}{70} \\ 1.4 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 140\% \end{aligned} }\)
\(98\) is \(140\%\) of \(70\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “\(98\) is what percent of \(70\text{,}\)”
  • \(98\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(70\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{98}{70} = 1.4 = 140\% }\)
\(98\) is \(140\%\) of \(70\text{.}\)
