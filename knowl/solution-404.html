Explanation 3.5.7.18.1.
When an equation of a line is written in the form \(y=mx+b\text{,}\) it is said to be in slope-intercept form. In this form, \(m\) is the line’s slope, and \(b\) is the coordinate on the \(y\)-axis where the line intercepts the \(y\)-axis.
In this problem, the line’s equation is given as \(\displaystyle{ y= 7+{10}x }\text{.}\) We may rewrite this in slope-intercept form: \(\displaystyle{ y= {10}x+7 }\text{.}\)
Thus the line’s slope is \({10}\text{,}\) and its \(y\)-intercept has coordinates \((0,7)\text{.}\)
