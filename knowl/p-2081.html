Kailey puts a pot of water from the tap onto the stove and turns the burner all the way up. The water temperature starts at \(61\,℉\) and climbs steadily up to the boiling point of \(212\,℉\text{,}\) raising at a rate of \(26\,\frac{℉}{\text{min}}\text{.}\) How long will it take for the pot to boil? Write an equation to model this scenario. There is no need to solve it.
