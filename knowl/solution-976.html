Explanation A.3.6.24.1.

First, recognize that \(100\) is a square number. Since \(10\cdot10=100\text{,}\) we have \(\sqrt{100}=10\text{.}\)
Next, since \(100\cdot100=10000\text{,}\) we have \(\sqrt{10000}=100\text{.}\)
Finally, since \(1000\cdot1000=1000000\text{,}\) we have \(\sqrt{1000000}=1000\text{.}\)
