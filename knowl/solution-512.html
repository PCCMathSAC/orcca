Explanation 3.7.11.1.

  1. To find the \(x\)-intercept:
    \begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} 2x+4.3y\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ 2x+4.3(\substitute{0})\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ 2x\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ x\amp=\frac{500}{99} \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
    So the \(x\)-intercept is at \(\left(\frac{500}{99},0\right)\text{.}\)
  2. To find the \(y\)-intercept:
    \begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} 2x+4.3y\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ 2(\substitute{0})+4.3y\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ 4.3y\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ y\amp=\multiplyleft{\frac{1}{4.3}}\frac{1000}{99}\\ y\amp\approx2.349\ldots \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
    So the \(y\)-intercept is at about \((0,2.349)\text{.}\)
  3. Since we have the \(x\)- and \(y\)-intercepts, we can calculate the slope:
    \begin{equation*} m\approx-\frac{2.349}{\frac{500}{99}}=-\frac{2.349\cdot99}{500}\approx-0.4561 \text{.} \end{equation*}
