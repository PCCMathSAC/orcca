1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{t}{9} \amp = 8 \\ 9 \cdot \frac{t}{9} \amp = 9 \cdot 8 \\ t \amp = 72 \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \(72\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(72\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{r}{p} \amp =\; A \\ p \cdot \frac{r}{p} \amp =\; p \cdot A \\ r \amp =\; {pA} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({pA}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({pA}\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
