Explanation 2.4.4.5.1.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} -8C + 10 \amp = -C -46 \\ -8C + 10\mathbf{{}+8C +46} \amp = -C -46\mathbf{{}+8C +46} \\ 56 \amp = 7C \\ \frac{56}{7} \amp = \frac{7C}{7} \\ 8 \amp = C\\ C \amp = 8 \end{aligned} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(8\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(C\text{,}\) some report \(C = 8\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{8\}\text{,}\) or that \(C\in\left\{8\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(8\) in for \(C\) in the original equation \(-8C+10=-C-46\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
