\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} -6r - 5 \amp = {49} \\ -6r - 5 \mathbf{{}+5} \amp = {49}\mathbf{{}+5} \\ -6r \amp = {54} \\ \frac{-6r}{-6} \amp = \frac{{54}}{-6} \\ r \amp = -9 \end{aligned} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(-9\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(r\text{,}\) some report \(r = -9\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{-9\}\text{,}\) or that \(r\in\left\{-9\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(-9\) in for \(r\) in the original equation \(-6r-5=49\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
