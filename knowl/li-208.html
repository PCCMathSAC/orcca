Item 2.5.3.9.a.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] t + 1 \amp = 5 \\ t + 1\mathbf{{} -1} \amp = 5\mathbf{{} -1} \\ t \amp = 4 \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \(4\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(4\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
