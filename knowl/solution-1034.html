Explanation A.4.3.29.1.

Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(7\) is \(x\%\) of \(37\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(7\) out of \(37\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{7}{37} \\ 37x \amp = 7 \cdot 100 \\ 37x \amp = 700 \\ \frac{37x}{37} \amp = \frac{700}{37} \\ x \amp \approx 18.9189189189189 \\ x \amp \approx 18.92 \end{aligned} }\)
\(7\) is approximately \(18.92\%\) of \(37\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to math should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(7\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(37\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 7 \amp = x \cdot 37 \\ \frac{7}{37} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 37}{37} \\ 0.189189189189189 \amp \approx x\\ x \amp \approx 18.92\% \end{aligned} }\)
\(7\) is approximately \(18.92\%\) of \(37\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “\(7\) is what percent of \(37\text{,}\)”
  • \(7\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(37\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{7}{37} \approx 0.189189189189189 \approx 18.92\% }\)
\(7\) is approximately \(18.92\%\) of \(37\text{.}\)
