Water straight out of the tap is \(59\) degrees Fahrenheit, and you fill a tea kettle. You place the tea kettle on an old stove burner and turn on the burner, but the heating element is old and not working well. At best, it begins raising the temperature inside the kettle at a rate of \(30\) degrees Fahrenheit per minute. How long will it take to boil the water? (Water boils at \(212\) degrees Fahrenheit.)
