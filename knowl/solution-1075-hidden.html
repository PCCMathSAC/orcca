The house’s value two years ago was \({\$120{,}000}\text{.}\) After one year, the house’s value decreased by \(7\%\text{.}\)
The amount of decrease was \(7\%\) of \({\$120{,}000}\text{.}\) There are two methods to find this amount.
We can use proportion. Let \(x\) dollars be the amount of decrease, then we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{120000} \amp = \frac{7}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 120000\cdot7 \\ 100x \amp = 840000 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{840000}{100} \\ x \amp = 8400 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of decrease:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.07 \cdot 120000 = 8400 }\)
After one year, the house’s value became \({\$120{,}000}-{\$8{,}400}={\$111{,}600}\text{.}\)
After another year, the house’s value decreased by another \(7\%\text{.}\)
The amount of decrease was \(7\%\) of \({\$111{,}600}\text{.}\) There are two methods to find this amount.
We can use proportion. Let \(x\) dollars be the amount of decrease, then we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{111600} \amp = \frac{7}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 111600\cdot7 \\ 100x \amp = 781200 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{781200}{100} \\ x \amp = 7812 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of decrease:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.07 \cdot 111600 = 7812 }\)
After two years, the house’s value became \({\$111{,}600}-{\$7{,}812}={\$103{,}788}\text{.}\)
The house’s value this year is \({\$103{,}788}\text{.}\)
