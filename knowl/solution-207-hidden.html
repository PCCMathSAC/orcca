1. \(\begin{aligned} 4t +6 \amp = 34 \\ 4t +6\mathbf{{}-6} \amp = 34\mathbf{{}-6} \\ 4t \amp = 28 \\ \frac{4t}{4} \amp = \frac{28}{4} \\ t \amp = 7 \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \(7\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(7\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] qr+p \amp = m \\ qr +p\mathbf{{}-p} \amp =m\mathbf{{}-p} \\ qr \amp = m-p \\ \frac{qr}{q} \amp = \frac{m-p}{q} \\ r \amp = {\frac{m-p}{q}} \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({\frac{m-p}{q}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{m-p}{q}}\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
