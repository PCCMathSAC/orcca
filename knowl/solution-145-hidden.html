\(\displaystyle{ \begin{alignedat}{2} 8a + 4 \amp = {68} \\ 8a + 4\mathbf{{}-4}\amp = {68} \mathbf{{}-4} \\ 8a \amp = {64} \\ \frac{8a}{8} \amp = \frac{{64}}{8} \\ a \amp = 8 \end{alignedat} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(8\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(a\text{,}\) some report \(a = 8\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{8\}\text{,}\) or that \(a\in\left\{8\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(8\) in for \(a\) in the original equation \(8a+4=68\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
