Item 3.6.5.59.b.
In one month, you spent \(200\) minutes on the phone. To find out the bill, we substitute \(200\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.07(x-230)+31.1\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.07(200-230) +31.1 \\ y \amp = 29 \end{aligned} }\)
If you spent \(200\) minutes over the phone in a month, your bill for that month would be \({\$29.00}\text{.}\)
