Item 3.6.5.64.b.
In one month, you spent \(190\) minutes on the phone. To find out the bill, we substitute \(190\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.035(x-240)+26.4\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.035(190-240) +26.4 \\ y \amp = 24.65 \end{aligned} }\)
If you spent \(190\) minutes over the phone in a month, your bill for that month would be \({\$24.65}\text{.}\)
