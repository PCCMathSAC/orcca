Explanation A.4.3.55.1.

First, we find the total number of students who participated in this survey:
\(\displaystyle{ 74+86+52+75+62=349 }\)
Now, this problem can be boiled down to this question: \(74\) is what percent of \(349\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(74\) is \(x\%\) of \(349\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(74\) out of \(349\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{74}{349} \\ 349x \amp = 74 \cdot 100 \\ 349x \amp = 7400 \\ \frac{349x}{349} \amp = \frac{7400}{349} \\ x \amp \approx 21.2034383954155 \\ x \amp \approx 21\% \end{aligned} }\)
Approximately \(21\) of students ride Bus #1.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(74\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(349\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 74 \amp = x \cdot 349 \\ \frac{74}{349} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 349}{349} \\ 0.212034383954155 \amp \approx x \\ x \amp \approx 21\% \end{aligned} }\)
Approximately \(21\) of students ride Bus #1.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(74\) is what percent of \(349\text{,}\)”
  • \(74\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(349\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{74}{349} \approx 0.212034383954155 \approx 21\% }\)
Approximately \(21\) of students ride Bus #1.
