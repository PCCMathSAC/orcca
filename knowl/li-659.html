Item 3.6.5.62.b.
After \(39\) minutes, we can find the mass of the remaining gas if we substitute \(39\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-5.4(x-8)+183.6\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -5.4(39-8) +183.6 \\ y \amp = 16.2 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(39\) minutes, there are \(16.2\) grams of gas remaining.
