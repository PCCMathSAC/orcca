Item 2.5.3.23.b.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] y+t \amp = q \\ y+t\mathbf{{}-y} \amp = q\mathbf{{}-y} \\ t \amp = {q-y} \\ \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({q-y}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({q-y}\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
