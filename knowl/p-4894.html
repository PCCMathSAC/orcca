In a time-distance graph, the slope of a line represents speed. The slopes in these examples and the running speeds of these runners are measured in m⁄s. A relationship we can see is that the more sharply a line is slanted, the bigger the slope is. This should make sense because for each passing second, the faster person travels longer, making a slope triangle’s height taller. This means that, numerically, we can tell that Cleo is the fastest runner (and Effie is the slowest) just by comparing the slopes \(4>3.5>2.666\text{.}\)
