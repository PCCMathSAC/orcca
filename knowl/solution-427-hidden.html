Let \(x\) be the number of T-shirts the school purchased.
Each T-shirt costs \({\$7}\text{,}\) so \(x\) T-shirts cost \(7x\) dollars. After that expense, there was a rebate of 75 dollars.
Now we can write an equation:
\(\displaystyle{ 7x-75 = 3145 }\)
Next, we solve for \(x\text{:}\)
\(\begin{aligned} 7x -75 \amp = 3145 \\ 7x -75\mathbf{{}+75} \amp = 3145\mathbf{{}+75} \\ 7x \amp = 3220 \\ \frac{7x}{7} \amp = \frac{3220}{7} \\ x \amp = 460 \end{aligned}\)
The school purchased \(460\) T-shirts.
