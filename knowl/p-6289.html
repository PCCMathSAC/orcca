  1. A line’s equation in slope-intercept form looks like \(y=mx+b\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \(y\) is the \(y\)-coordinate of the \(y\)-intercept. We first need to find the line’s slope. To find the slope, we need two points on the line. We have been given that \((12,19.32)\) and \((18,20.88)\) are points on the linear model, so we can use the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \text{slope}=\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}} }\)
    First, we mark which number corresponds to which variable in the formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ (12,19.32) \longrightarrow (x_{1},y_{1}) }\)
    \(\displaystyle{ (18,20.88) \longrightarrow (x_{2},y_{2}) }\)
    Now we substitute these numbers into the corresponding variables in the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}\text{slope}\amp =\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\\\amp =\frac{20.88-19.32}{18-12}\\\amp =\frac{1.56}{6}\\\amp =0.26 \end{aligned}}\)
    This line’s slope is \(0.26\text{.}\) This implies that the tree is growing by \(0.26\) feet each month.
    So now we have that \(y=0.26x+b\text{.}\) The next step is to find the value of \(b\text{.}\) There are at least two ways.
    One way is to substitute one of the given points into \(y=0.26x+b\text{.}\) We choose to use \((12,19.32)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.26x + b \\ 19.32 \amp = 0.26 \cdot 12 + b \\ 19.32 \amp = 3.12 + b \\ 19.32\mathbf{{}-3.12} \amp = 3.12 + b\mathbf{{}-3.12} \\ 16.2 \amp = b\\ b \amp = 16.2 \end{aligned}\)
    This line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=0.26x+16.2\text{.}\)
    Another way is to use the line’s point-slope equation \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\text{.}\) Again, we choose to use the point \((12,19.32)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y-y_{1} \amp = m(x-x_{1}) \\ y-19.32 \amp = 0.26(x-12) \\ y-19.32 \amp = 0.26x+0.26 \cdot (-12) \\ y-19.32 \amp = 0.26x - 3.12 \\ y-19.32\mathbf{{}+19.32} \amp = 0.26x - 3.12\mathbf{{}+19.32} \\ y \amp = 0.26x+16.2 \end{aligned}\)
    This is the second way to find \(b\text{,}\) and thus the line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=0.26x+16.2\text{.}\)
  2. After \(28\) months, we can find the height of the tree if we substitute \(28\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.26x+16.2\text{.}\) We have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.26x+16.2 \\ y \amp = 0.26 \cdot 28 +16.2 \\ y \amp = 7.28+16.2 \\ y \amp = 23.48 \end{aligned} }\)
    So after \(28\) months, the tree is \(23.48\) feet tall.
  3. To find when the tree was \(30.76\) feet tall, we can substitute \(30.76\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.26x+16.2\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.26x+16.2 \\ 30.76 \amp = 0.26x +16.2 \\ 30.76\mathbf{{}-16.2} \amp = 0.26x +16.2\mathbf{{}-16.2}\\ 14.56 \amp = 0.26x \\ \frac{14.56}{0.26} \amp = \frac{0.26x}{0.26} \\ 56 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    So the tree was \(30.76\) feet tall after \(56\) months.
