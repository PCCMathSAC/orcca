  1. A line’s equation in point-slope form looks like \(y=m(x-x_0)+y_0\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \((x_0,y_0)\) is some point we know to be on the line. We first need to find the line’s slope. We have been told that the account decreases by \({\$50{,}000}\) each year, so the slope of the linear model is \(-50000\) (dollars per year).
    So now we have that \(y=-50000(x-x_0)+y_0\text{.}\) We can use \((x_0,y_0)=(4,473000)\) to get \({y = -50000\mathopen{}\left(x-4\right)+473000}\text{.}\)
  2. In the year \(2011\text{,}\) \(x\) was \(11\text{.}\) To find the amount remaining in the fund, we substitute \(11\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-50000(x-4)+473000\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -50000(11-4) +473000 \\ y \amp = 123000 \end{aligned} }\)
    So in the year \(2011\text{,}\) the fund had \({\$123{,}000}\) remaining in it.
  3. The fund will be empty when it has \({\$0}\) left in it. That is, \(y\) will equal \(0\text{.}\) To find how many years until this happens, we substitute \(0\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=-50000(x-4)+473000\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -50000(x-4) +473000 \\ 0 \amp = -50000(x-4) +473000 \\ -473000 \amp = -50000(x-4)\\ \frac{-473000}{-50000} \amp = x - 4\\ 9.46 \amp = x - 4\\ 13.46 \amp = x\\ 13.46 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    Approximately \(13.46\) years after 2000, in the year 2013, the fund will be depleted.
