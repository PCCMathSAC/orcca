For this problem, we are trying to find \({{\frac{5}{6}}}\) of \({{\frac{9}{10}}}\text{.}\) The word “of” implies multiplication:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{5}{6} \amp = \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \\ \amp = \frac{3\cdot1}{2\cdot2} \\ \amp = {{\frac{3}{4}}} \end{aligned} }\)
So \({{\frac{3}{4}}}\) of the grant will be used to buy new equipment.
