Item 2.5.3.13.b.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] -y + n \amp = p \\ -y + n\mathbf{{} -n} \amp = p\mathbf{{} -n} \\ -y \amp = p-n \\ (-1) \cdot (-y) \amp =(-1) \cdot (p-n) \\ (-1) \cdot (-y) \amp =(-1) \cdot (p+(-n)) \\ y \amp =(-1) \cdot p + (-1) \cdot (-n) \\ y \amp = -p+n \quad (\text{already correct}) \\ y \amp =n-p \quad (\text{looks better}) \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({n-p}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({n-p}\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
