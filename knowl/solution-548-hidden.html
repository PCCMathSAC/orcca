1. Let’s put \(Ax + By = C\) into slope-intercept form. Then, the equation becomes \(y = \frac{-A}{B}x + \frac{C}{B} \text{.}\) To make the steepest slope possible, we must maximize the fraction \(\frac{A}{B}\text{.}\) The possibilities are \(\frac{6}{7}, \frac{6}{15}, \frac{7}{6}, \frac{7}{15}, \frac{15}{6}\text{,}\) and \(\frac{15}{7} \text{.}\) The biggest of these is \(\frac{15}{6} \text{.}\) Thus, \(A\) must equal 15, \(B\) must equal 6, and \(C\) must equal 7.
  2. To make the shallowest slope possible, we must minimize the fraction \(\frac{A}{B}\text{.}\) The smallest of the possible fractions is \(\frac{6}{15} \text{.}\) Thus, \(A\) must equal 6, \(B\) must equal 15, and \(C\) must equal 7.
