Item 2.5.3.17.b.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{t}{c} \amp =\; n \\ c \cdot \frac{t}{c} \amp =\; c \cdot n \\ t \amp =\; {cn} \\ \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({cn}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({cn}\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
