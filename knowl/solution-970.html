Explanation A.3.6.18.1.

You can quickly find the first few square numbers on scratch paper:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] 1^{2} \amp = 1 \\ 2^{2} \amp = 4 \\ 3^{2} \amp = 9 \\ 4^{2} \amp = 16 \\ 5^{2} \amp = 25 \\ 6^{2} \amp = 36 \\ 7^{2} \amp = 49 \\ 8^{2} \amp = 64 \\ 9^{2} \amp = 81 \\ 10^{2} \amp = 100 \\ 11^{2} \amp = 121 \\ 12^{2} \amp = 144 \\ \end{aligned} }\)
It would be very helpful if you can memorize the above 12 square numbers.
So the correct answers are:
  1. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle{\sqrt{4} = 2}\)
  2. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle{\sqrt{100} = 10}\)
  3. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle{\sqrt{121} = 11}\)
