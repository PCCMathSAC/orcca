First, we need to find how much tip Huynh paid. This problem can be boiled down to this question: What is \(19\%\) of \(22\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(19\%\) of \(22\) is \(x\text{,}\) so “\(19\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(x\) out of \(22\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{19}{100} \amp = \frac{x}{22} \\ 100x \amp = 19 \cdot 22 \\ 100x \amp = 418 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{418}{100} \\ x \amp = 4.18 \end{aligned} }\)
Huynh will pay \({\$4.18}\) in tip. In total, he will pay \({\$22.00}+{\$4.18}={\$26.18}\text{.}\)
Huynh will pay \({\$26.18}\) in total (including bill and tip).
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: What is \(19\%\) of \(22\text{?}\) Assume \(x\) is \(19\%\) of \(22\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} x \amp = 0.19 \cdot 22 \\ \amp = 4.18 \end{aligned} }\)
Huynh will pay \({\$4.18}\) in tip. In total, he will pay \({\$22.00}+{\$4.18}={\$26.18}\text{.}\)
Huynh will pay \({\$26.18}\) in total (including bill and tip).
Method 3
In the sentence “What is \(19\%\) of \(22\text{,}\)”
  • “what” is the percentage,
  • \(19\%\) is the rate,
  • \(22\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{percentage} = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base}\text{,}\) we do a multiplication to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{percentage } = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base} = 19\% \cdot 22 = 0.19 \cdot 22 = 4.18 }\)
Huynh will pay \({\$4.18}\) in tip. In total, he will pay \({\$22.00}+{\$4.18}={\$26.18}\text{.}\)
Huynh will pay \({\$26.18}\) in total (including bill and tip).
