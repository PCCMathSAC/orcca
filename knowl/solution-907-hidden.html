To subtract two fractions with the same denominator, we simply subtract the numerators and keep the denominator unchanged.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} \frac{25}{16} - \frac{15}{16} \amp = \frac{25 - 15}{16} \\ \amp = \frac{10}{16} \amp \hbox{We can reduce this by dividing 2 into both the numerator and the denominator. }\\ \amp = \frac{5}{8} \\ \end{aligned} }\)
