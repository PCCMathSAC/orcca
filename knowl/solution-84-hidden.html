That first subtraction may be tricky. It may help to write that as addition of \(-1\) times the rest:
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} 4-(3x-9) \amp=4+(-1)(3x-9)\\ \amp=4+\Big(-1(3x)-(-1)(9)\Big)\\ \amp=4+(-3x+9)\\ \amp=4+(9-3x)\\ \amp=4+(9+(-3x))\\ \amp=(4+9)+(-3x)\\ \amp=13+(-3x)=13-3x \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
