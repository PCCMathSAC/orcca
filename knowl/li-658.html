Item 3.6.5.62.a.
A line’s equation in point-slope form looks like \(y=m(x-x_0)+y_0\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \((x_0,y_0)\) is some point we know to be on the line. We first need to find the line’s slope. Since the gas is leaking at a rate of \(5.4\) grams per minute, we know that the slope of the linear model is \(-5.4\text{.}\)
So now we have that \(y=-5.4(x-x_0)+y_0\text{.}\) We can use \((x_0,y_0)=(8,183.6)\) to get \({y = -5.4\mathopen{}\left(x-8\right)+183.6}\text{.}\)
