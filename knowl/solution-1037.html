Explanation A.4.3.32.1.

It’s given there are \(33\%\) Democrats, \(29\%\) Republicans, and the rest are Independents. Now we can use subtraction to find the percentage of registered Independent residents:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{{}=}1-33\%-29\% \\ \amp =1-0.33-0.29 \\ \amp =0.38 \\ \amp = 38\% \end{aligned} }\)
So there are \(38\%\) Independents. Now this problem can be boiled down to this question: What is \(38\%\) of \(3600\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(38\%\) of \(3600\) is \(x\text{,}\) so “\(38\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(x\) out of \(3600\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{38}{100} \amp = \frac{x}{3600} \\ 100x \amp = 38 \cdot 3600 \\ 100x \amp = 136800 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{136800}{100} \\ x \amp = 1368 \end{aligned} }\)
There are \(1368\) registered Independent residents in this town.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: What is \(38\%\) of \(3600\text{?}\) Assume \(x\) is \(38\%\) of \(3600\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} x \amp = 0.38 \cdot 3600 \\ \amp = 1368 \end{aligned} }\)
There are \(1368\) registered Independent residents in this town.
Method 3
In the sentence “What is \(38\%\) of \(3600\text{,}\)”
  • “what” is the percentage,
  • \(38\%\) is the rate,
  • \(3600\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{percentage} = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base}\text{,}\) we do a multiplication to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{percentage } = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base} = 38\% \cdot 3600 = 0.38 \cdot 3600 = 1368 }\)
There are \(1368\) registered Independent residents in this town.
