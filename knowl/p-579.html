A metals recycling plant makes its revenue from selling aluminum at \(A\) dollars per ton, steel at \(S\) dollars per ton, and tin at \(T\) dollars per ton. One week, they processed \(3.42\) tons of aluminum, \(3.95\) tons of steel, and \(3.16\) tons of tin. So they generated \({3.42A+3.95S+3.16T}\) dollars in revenue.
