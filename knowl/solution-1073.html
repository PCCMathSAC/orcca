Explanation A.4.3.68.1.

Question 1
Your original salary was \({\$46{,}000}\) per year. The amount of cut was \(4\%\) of \({\$46{,}000}\text{.}\) There are two methods to find this amount.
We can use proportion. Let \(x\) dollars be the amount of decrease, then we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{46000} \amp = \frac{4}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 46000\cdot4 \\ 100x \amp = 184000 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{184000}{100} \\ x \amp = 1840 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of increase:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.04 \cdot {\$46{,}000} = {\$1{,}840} }\)
After the cut, your salary became \({\$46{,}000}-{\$1{,}840} = {\$44{,}160}\) dollars per year.
Question 2
Next, the amount of pay raise was \(4\%\) of \({\$44{,}160}\text{.}\) Notice that it’s incorrect to find \(4\%\) of \({\$46{,}000}\text{,}\) because the annual salary has changed from \({\$46{,}000}\) to \({\$44{,}160}\text{.}\)
We will first use proportion to find the amount of pay cut. Assume the amount of pay cut was \(x\) dollars. We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{44160} \amp = \frac{4}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 44160\cdot4 \\ 100x \amp = 176640 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{176640}{100} \\ x \amp = 1766.4 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of pay cut:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.04 \cdot {\$44{,}160} = {\$1{,}766.40} }\)
After the raise, your salary became \({\$44{,}160}+{\$1{,}766.40} = {\$45{,}926.40}\) per year.
After a cut and a raise of the same rate, your salary decreased! This is because the rate of cut was based on a bigger salary, while the rate of raise was based on a smaller salary (after the cut).
