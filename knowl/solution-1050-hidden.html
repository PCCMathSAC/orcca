This problem can be boiled down to this question: \(7800\) is \(60\%\) of what? We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(7800\) is \(60\%\) of \(x\text{,}\) so “\(60\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(7800\) out of \(x\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{60}{100} \amp = \frac{7800}{x} \\ 60x \amp = 100 \cdot 7800 \\ 60x \amp = 780000 \\ \frac{60x}{60} \amp = \frac{780000}{60} \\ x \amp = 13000 \end{aligned} }\)
This county has \(13000\) residents.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: \(7800\) is \(60\%\) of what? Assume \(7800\) is \(60\%\) of \(x\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} 7800 \amp = 0.6 \cdot x \\ \frac{7800}{0.6} \amp = \frac{0.6x}{0.6} \\ 13000 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
This county has \(13000\) residents.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(7800\) is \(60\%\) of what,”
  • \(7800\) is the percentage,
  • \(60\%\) is the rate,
  • “what” is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{base} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{rate}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{base} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{rate}} = \frac{7800}{60\%} = \frac{7800}{0.6} = 13000 }\)
This county has \(13000\) residents.
