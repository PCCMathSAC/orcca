To calculate the percentage increase/decrease, first we find the amount of increase/decrease by doing a simple subtraction calculation, and then we find the percentage increase/decrease.
In this problem, the amount of increase is \(27-14\text{,}\) which is \(13\text{.}\)
Next, since we started with \(14\) cats, we need to ask: \(13\) is what percent of \(14\text{?}\)
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(13\) is \(x\%\) of \(14\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(13\) out of \(14\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{13}{14} \\ 14x \amp = 13 \cdot 100 \\ 14x \amp = 1300 \\ \frac{14x}{14} \amp = \frac{1300}{14} \\ x \amp \approx 92.8571428571429 \\ x \amp \approx 92.86 \end{aligned} }\)
The percentage increase of the shelter’s cat population is approximately \(92.86\%\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Let the increase \(13\) be \(x\) (as a percent) of \(14\text{.}\) That means:
\(\begin{aligned} 13 \amp = x \cdot 14 \\ \frac{13}{14} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 14}{14} \\ 0.928571428571429 \amp \approx x\\ x \amp \approx 92.86\%\\ \end{aligned}\)
The percentage increase of the shelter’s cat population is approximately \(92.86\%\text{.}\)
Method 3
We first divide the “new number” by the “original number”:
\(\displaystyle{ \frac{27}{14} \approx 1.92857142857143 \approx 192.86\% }\)
So the new number is approximately \(192.86\%\) of the original number, implying the percentage increase is approximately \(192.86\% - 100\% = 92.86\%\text{.}\)
The percentage increase of the shelter’s cat population is approximately \(92.86\%\text{.}\)
