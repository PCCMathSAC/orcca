Item 3.6.5.61.b.
After \(30\) months, we can find the height of the tree if we substitute \(30\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.11(x-10)+18.8\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.11(30-10) +18.8 \\ y \amp = 21 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(30\) months, the tree is \(\) feet tall.
