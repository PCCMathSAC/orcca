Jay’s pay is made up of base pay and commission. In that month, Jay earned a total of \({\$3{,}849.11}\text{,}\) with \({\$1{,}100.00}\) of base pay. This implies Jay earned \({\$3{,}849.11}-{\$1{,}100.00}={\$2{,}749.11}\) in commission, out of \({\$74{,}100.00}\) in sales.
Now the problem can be boiled down to this question: \(2749.11\) is what percent of \(74100\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(2749.11\) is \(x\%\) of \(74100\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(2749.11\) out of \(74100\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{2749.11}{74100} \\ 74100x \amp = 2749.11 \cdot 100 \\ 74100x \amp = 274911 \\ \frac{74100x}{74100} \amp = \frac{274911}{74100} \\ x \amp = 3.71 \end{aligned} }\)
Jay earned \(3.71\%\) in commission.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(2749.11\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(74100\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 2749.11 \amp = x \cdot 74100 \\ \frac{2749.11}{74100} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 74100}{74100} \\ 0.0371 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 3.71\% \end{aligned} }\)
Jay earned \(3.71\%\) in commission.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(2749.11\) is what percent of \(74100\text{,}\)”
  • \(2749.11\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(74100\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{2749.11}{74100} = 0.0371 = 3.71\% }\)
Jay earned \(3.71\%\) in commission.
