Assume Hayden will purchase \(x\) tea bags and \(y\) sugar bags. Use a linear equation to model the number of tea bags and sugar bags he can purchase.
Find this line’s \(x\)-intercept, and interpret its meaning in this context.
  • A. The x-intercept is (0, 135). It implies Hayden can purchase 135 sugar bags with no tea bags.
  • B. The x-intercept is (135,0). It implies Hayden can purchase 135 tea bags with no sugar bags.
  • C. The x-intercept is (0,30). It implies Hayden can purchase 30 sugar bags with no tea bags.
  • D. The x-intercept is (30,0). It implies Hayden can purchase 30 tea bags with no sugar bags.
Answer.

An engine’s tank can hold \(150\) gallons of gasoline. It was refilled with a full tank, and has been running without breaks, consuming \(5\) gallons of gas per hour.
Assume the engine has been running for \(x\) hours since its tank was refilled, and assume there are \(y\) gallons of gas left in the tank. Use a linear equation to model the amount of gas in the tank as time passes.
Find this line’s \(x\)-intercept, and interpret its meaning in this context.
  • A. The x-intercept is (30,0). It implies the engine will run out of gas 30 hours after its tank was refilled.
  • B. The x-intercept is (150,0). It implies the engine will run out of gas 150 hours after its tank was refilled.
  • C. The x-intercept is (0,30). It implies the engine started with 30 gallons of gas in its tank.
  • D. The x-intercept is (0,150). It implies the engine started with 150 gallons of gas in its tank.
Answer.
