  1. A line’s equation in point-slope form looks like \(y=m(x-x_0)+y_0\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \((x_0,y_0)\) is some point we know to be on the line. We first need to find the line’s slope. The slope of the linear model has been given to us: \({\$0.07}\) per minute.
    So now we have that \(y=0.07(x-x_0)+y_0\text{.}\) We can use \((x_0,y_0)=(230,31.1)\) to get \({y = 0.07\mathopen{}\left(x-230\right)+31.1}\text{.}\)
  2. In one month, you spent \(200\) minutes on the phone. To find out the bill, we substitute \(200\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.07(x-230)+31.1\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.07(200-230) +31.1 \\ y \amp = 29 \end{aligned} }\)
    If you spent \(200\) minutes over the phone in a month, your bill for that month would be \({\$29.00}\text{.}\)
  3. In one month, you had a cell phone bill of \({\$50.00}\text{.}\) To find how many minutes you spent on the phone that month, we substitute \(50\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.07(x-230)+31.1\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.07(x-230) +31.1 \\ 50 \amp = 0.07(x-230) +31.1 \\ 18.9 \amp = 0.07(x-230)\\ \frac{18.9}{0.07} \amp = x - 230\\ 270 \amp = x - 230\\ 500 \amp = x\\ 500 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    If your bill was \({\$50.00}\) in one month, you must have spent \(500\) minutes on the phone.
