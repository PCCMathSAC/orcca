To multiply two fractions, simply multiply both numerators and both denominators. We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] \frac{4}{9} \cdot \frac{4}{7} \amp = \frac{4 \cdot 4}{9 \cdot 7} \\ \amp = \frac{16}{63} \end{aligned} }\)
The answer to this question is \(\displaystyle{\frac{16}{63}}\text{.}\)
