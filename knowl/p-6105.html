  1. A line’s equation in slope-intercept form looks like \(y=mx+b\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \(y\) is the \(y\)-coordinate of the \(y\)-intercept. The slope of the linear model has been given to us: \({\$0.07}\) per minute.
    So now we have that \(y=0.07x+b\text{.}\) The next step is to find the value of \(b\text{.}\) There are at least two ways.
    One way is to substitute a given point into \(y={0.07x}+b\text{.}\) We have the point \((250,28.5)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y \amp = {0.07x} + b \\ 28.5 \amp = 0.07 \cdot 250 + b \\ 28.5 \amp = 17.5 + b \\ 28.5\mathbf{{}-17.5} \amp = 17.5 + b\mathbf{{}-17.5} \\ 11 \amp = b\\ b \amp = 11 \end{aligned}\)
    This line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y={0.07x}+11\text{.}\)
    Another way is to use the line’s point-slope equation \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\text{.}\) Again, we use the point \((250,28.5)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y-y_{1} \amp = m(x-x_{1}) \\ y-28.5 \amp = 0.07(x-250) \\ y-28.5 \amp = {0.07x}+0.07 \cdot (-250) \\ y-28.5 \amp = {0.07x} - 17.5 \\ y-28.5\mathbf{{}+28.5} \amp = {0.07x} - 17.5\mathbf{{}+28.5} \\ y \amp = {0.07x}+11 \end{aligned}\)
    This is the second way to find \(b\text{,}\) and thus the line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=0.07x+11\text{.}\)
  2. In one month, you spent \(180\) minutes on the phone. To find out the bill, we substitute \(180\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.07x+11\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.07x+11 \\ y \amp = 0.07 \cdot 180 +11 \\ y \amp = 12.6+11 \\ y \amp = 23.6 \end{aligned} }\)
    If you spent \(180\) minutes over the phone in a month, your bill for that month would be \({\$23.60}\text{.}\)
  3. In one month, you had a cell phone bill of \({\$39.70}\text{.}\) To find how many minutes you spent on the phone that month, we substitute \(39.7\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.07x+11\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.07x+11 \\ 39.7 \amp = 0.07x +11 \\ 39.7\mathbf{{}-11} \amp = 0.07x +11\mathbf{{}-11}\\ 28.7 \amp = 0.07x \\ \frac{28.7}{0.07} \amp = \frac{0.07x}{0.07} \\ 410 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    If your bill was \({\$39.70}\) in one month, you must have spent \(410\) minutes on the phone.
