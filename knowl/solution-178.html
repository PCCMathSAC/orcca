Explanation 2.4.4.35.1.
\(\begin{aligned} 10(k-6) \amp \leq 10(k-2) \\ 10k-10 \cdot 6 \amp \leq 10k-10 \cdot 2 \\ 10k-60 \amp \leq 10k-20 \\ 10k-60\mathbf{{} -10k+60} \amp \leq 10k-20\mathbf{{} -10k+60} \\ 0 \amp \leq 40 \end{aligned}\)
Since \(0 \leq 40\) is true, the solution set for this inequality is all real numbers.
