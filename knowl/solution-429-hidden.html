Assume after \(y\) years, this country will have \(345.84\) million acres of forest left.
The country loses \(7.86\) million acres of forest every year. In \(y\) years, the country will lose a total of \(7.86y\) million acres of forest.
Currently, the country has \(699.54\) million acres of forest. In \(y\) years, with a loss of \(7.86y\) million acres, there would be \(699.54-7.86y\) million acres of forest left.
The question asks when there would be \(345.84\) million acres of forest left. Now we can write an equation:
\(\displaystyle{ 699.54-7.86y = 345.84 }\)
Next, we solve for \(y\text{:}\)
\(\begin{aligned} 699.54-7.86y \amp = 345.84 \\ 699.54-7.86y \mathbf{{}-699.54} \amp = 345.84 \mathbf{{}-699.54} \\ -7.86y \amp = -353.7 \\ \frac{-7.86y}{-7.86} \amp = \frac{-353.7}{-7.86} \\ y \amp = {45} \end{aligned}\)
After \({45}\) years, this country would have \(345.84\) million acres of forest left.
