Item 2.5.3.14.a.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] -y + 9 \amp = -1 \\ -y + 9\mathbf{{}-9} \amp = -1\mathbf{{}-9} \\ -y \amp = -10 \\ (-1) \cdot (-y) \amp = (-1) \cdot (-10) \\ y \amp = 10 \\ \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \(10\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(10\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
