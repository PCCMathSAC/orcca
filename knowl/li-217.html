Item 2.5.3.11.b.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] x - q \amp = -3 \\ x - q\mathbf{{} +q} \amp = -3\mathbf{{} +q} \\ x \amp = {q-3} \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({q-3}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({q-3}\) into the original equation for \(x\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
