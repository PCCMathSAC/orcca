Explanation A.2.7.80.1.

This is a subtraction problem.
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {{\frac{17}{35}}} - {{\frac{2}{7}}} \amp = \frac{17}{35}-\frac{2 \cdot 5}{7 \cdot 5} \\ \amp = \frac{17}{35} - \frac{10}{35} \\ \amp = \frac{17-10}{35} \\ \amp = \frac{7}{35} \\ \amp = {{\frac{1}{5}}} \end{aligned} }\)
The second leg is \({{\frac{1}{5}}}\) of a mile in length.
