When we use the word “of” in situations like “\({{\frac{1}{3}}}\) of \({\$10{,}800}\text{,}\)” we can translate “of” into the multiplication symbol.
In this problem, to find \({{\frac{1}{3}}}\) of \({\$10{,}800}\text{,}\) we do:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {{\frac{1}{3}}} \cdot 10800 \amp = {{\frac{1}{3}}} \cdot \frac{10800}{1} \\ \amp = \frac{1}{1} \cdot \frac{3600}{1} \\ \amp = 3600 \end{aligned} }\)
The car is worth \({\$3{,}600}\) now.
