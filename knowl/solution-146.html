Explanation 2.4.4.3.1.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} -7c - 4 \amp = {52} \\ -7c - 4 \mathbf{{}+4} \amp = {52}\mathbf{{}+4} \\ -7c \amp = {56} \\ \frac{-7c}{-7} \amp = \frac{{56}}{-7} \\ c \amp = -8 \end{aligned} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(-8\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(c\text{,}\) some report \(c = -8\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{-8\}\text{,}\) or that \(c\in\left\{-8\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(-8\) in for \(c\) in the original equation \(-7c-4=52\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
