To calculate the percentage increase/decrease, first we find the amount of increase/decrease by doing a simple subtraction calculation, and then we find the percentage increase/decrease.
In this problem, the amount of increase is \(29-22\text{,}\) which is \(7\text{.}\)
Next, since we started with \(22\) cats, we need to ask: \(7\) is what percent of \(22\text{?}\)
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(7\) is \(x\%\) of \(22\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(7\) out of \(22\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{7}{22} \\ 22x \amp = 7 \cdot 100 \\ 22x \amp = 700 \\ \frac{22x}{22} \amp = \frac{700}{22} \\ x \amp \approx 31.8181818181818 \\ x \amp \approx 31.82 \end{aligned} }\)
The percentage increase of the shelter’s cat population is approximately \(31.82\%\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Let the increase \(7\) be \(x\) (as a percent) of \(22\text{.}\) That means:
\(\begin{aligned} 7 \amp = x \cdot 22 \\ \frac{7}{22} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 22}{22} \\ 0.318181818181818 \amp \approx x\\ x \amp \approx 31.82\%\\ \end{aligned}\)
The percentage increase of the shelter’s cat population is approximately \(31.82\%\text{.}\)
Method 3
We first divide the “new number” by the “original number”:
\(\displaystyle{ \frac{29}{22} \approx 1.31818181818182 \approx 131.82\% }\)
So the new number is approximately \(131.82\%\) of the original number, implying the percentage increase is approximately \(131.82\% - 100\% = 31.82\%\text{.}\)
The percentage increase of the shelter’s cat population is approximately \(31.82\%\text{.}\)
