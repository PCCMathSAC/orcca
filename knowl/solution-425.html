Explanation 3.5.7.59.1.
Let \(x\) be the number of months since you became a member of the gym.
If you pay \({\$22}\) per month, you would pay \(22 \cdot x\) dollars for \(x\) months. In addition, you paid \({\$25}\) up front.
Now we can write an equation:
\(\displaystyle{ 25+22x = 465 }\)
Next, we solve for \(x\text{:}\)
\(\begin{aligned} 25 +22x \amp = 465 \\ 25 +22x\mathbf{{} -25} \amp = 465\mathbf{{} -25} \\ 22x \amp = 440 \\ \frac{22x}{22} \amp = \frac{440}{22} \\ x \amp = 20 \end{aligned}\)
\(20\) months have passed since you joined the gym.
