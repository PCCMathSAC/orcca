Considering the definition for rate of change, this means that when \(x\) and \(y\) are two variables where the rate of change between any two points is always the same, then you can calculate slope, \(m\text{,}\) by finding two distinct data points \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\text{,}\) and calculating
\begin{equation} m=\frac{\text{change in $y$}}{\text{change in $x$}}=\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\text{.}\tag{3.4.1} \end{equation}
