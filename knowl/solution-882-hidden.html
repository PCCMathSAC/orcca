To add two fractions with the same denominator, we simply add up the numerators and keep the denominator unchanged.
The result is:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} \frac{1}{20} + \frac{1}{20} \amp = \frac{1 + 1}{20} \\ \amp = \frac{2}{20} \amp \hbox{We can reduce this by dividing 2 into both the numerator and the denominator.}\\ \amp = \frac{1}{10} \\ \end{aligned} }\)
