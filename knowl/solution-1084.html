Explanation A.5.10.1.

The expression \((20-4^2)(4-6)^3\) has two sets of parentheses, so our first step will be to simplify what’s inside each of those first according to the order of operations. Once we’ve done that, we’ll apply the exponent and then finally divide:
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} (20-4^2)\div(4-6)^3 \amp= (20-\nextoperation{4^2})(4-6)^3\\ \amp= (\nextoperation{20-\highlight{16}})(4-6)^3\\ \amp= \highlight{4}(\nextoperation{4-6})^3\\ \amp= 4\nextoperation{(\highlight{-2})^3}\\ \amp= \nextoperation{4(\highlight{-8})}\\ \amp= \highlight{-32} \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
