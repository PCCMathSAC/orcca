Explanation A.1.7.51.1.

There is no operation symbols between \(0.065\) and \(0.57\text{,}\) which implies multiplication.
Next, since “negative times negative is positive,” the problem becomes:
\(\displaystyle{ (-0.065)(-0.57) = 0.065 \cdot 0.57 }\)
We know the answer must be positive.
It’s given that \(65\cdot57=3705\text{.}\) We need to calculate \(0.065 \cdot 0.57\text{.}\)
Notice that \(0.065=\frac{65}{1000}\) and \(0.57=\frac{57}{100}\text{,}\) so we can calculate \(0.065 \cdot 0.57\) in this way:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{{}=} 0.065 \cdot 0.57 \\ \amp = \frac{65}{1000} \cdot \frac{57}{100} \\ \amp = \frac{65\cdot57}{1000\cdot100} \\ \amp = \frac{3705}{100000} \\ \amp = 0.03705 \end{aligned} }\)
Once we understand the math, we can use this shortcut.
  1. To change \(65\) to \(0.065\text{,}\) we moved the decimal point to the left by \(3\) place(s).
  2. To change \(57\) to \(0.57\text{,}\) we moved the decimal point to the left by \(2\) place(s).
  3. We move the decimal point of \(3705\) to the left by \(3+2=5\) places to get \(0.065\cdot0.57=0.03705\text{.}\)
\(\displaystyle{ (-0.065)(-0.57) = 0.03705 }\)
