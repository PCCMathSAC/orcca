Item 3.5.7.70.b.
In one month, you spent \(170\) minutes on the phone. To find out the bill, we substitute \(170\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.045x+14\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.045x+14 \\ y \amp = 0.045 \cdot 170 +14 \\ y \amp = 7.65+14 \\ y \amp = 21.65 \end{aligned} }\)
If you spent \(170\) minutes over the phone in a month, your bill for that month would be \({\$21.65}\text{.}\)
