Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(14.4\) is \(x\%\) of \(12\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(14.4\) out of \(12\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{14.4}{12} \\ 12x \amp = 14.4 \cdot 100 \\ 12x \amp = 1440 \\ \frac{12x}{12} \amp = \frac{1440}{12} \\ x \amp = 120 \end{aligned} }\)
\(14.4\) is \(120\%\) of \(12\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(14.4\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(12\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 14.4 \amp = x \cdot 12 \\ \frac{14.4}{12} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 12}{12} \\ 1.2 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 120\% \end{aligned} }\)
\(14.4\) is \(120\%\) of \(12\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “\(14.4\) is what percent of \(12\text{,}\)”
  • \(14.4\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(12\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{14.4}{12} = 1.2 = 120\% }\)
\(14.4\) is \(120\%\) of \(12\text{.}\)
