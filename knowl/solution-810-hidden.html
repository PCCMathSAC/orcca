When doing decimal subtractions, make sure to line up the decimal points. For the integer \(85\text{,}\) the decimal point is at the end of the number, like in \(85.00\)
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{-}85.00 \\ \amp \underline{-\phantom{8}9.31\phantom{ad}} \\ \amp \phantom{-}75.69 \end{aligned} }\)
