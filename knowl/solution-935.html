Explanation A.2.7.94.1.

In this problem, the team maintains \({{\frac{5}{8}}}\) miles of sewage pipe every day, until they complete all \({95}\) miles. This is like repeatedly taking away \({{\frac{5}{8}}}\) miles from \({95}\) miles, a division problem.
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {95} \div {{\frac{5}{8}}} \amp = \frac{{95}}{1} \div \frac{5}{8} \\ \amp = \frac{{95}}{1} \cdot \frac{8}{5} \\ \amp = \frac{{19}}{1} \cdot \frac{8}{1} \\ \amp = \frac{{19} \cdot 8}{1 \cdot 1} \\ \amp = {152} \end{aligned} }\)
It will take the team \({152}\) days to complete maintaining the entire sewage pipe.
