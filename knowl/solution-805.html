Explanation A.1.7.40.1.

To subtract two decimals, make sure to line up the decimal points:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{-}6.30 \\ \amp \underline{-5.72\phantom{ad}} \\ \amp \phantom{-}0.58 \end{aligned} }\)
Note that we added an extra \(0\) to the end of \(6.3\) before doing subtraction.
