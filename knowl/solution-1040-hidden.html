First, we need to find how much tip Perlia paid. This problem can be boiled down to this question: What is \(12\%\) of \(50\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(12\%\) of \(50\) is \(x\text{,}\) so “\(12\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(x\) out of \(50\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{12}{100} \amp = \frac{x}{50} \\ 100x \amp = 12 \cdot 50 \\ 100x \amp = 600 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{600}{100} \\ x \amp = 6 \end{aligned} }\)
Perlia will pay \({\$6.00}\) in tip. In total, she will pay \({\$50.00}+{\$6.00}={\$56.00}\text{.}\)
Perlia will pay \({\$56.00}\) in total (including bill and tip).
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: What is \(12\%\) of \(50\text{?}\) Assume \(x\) is \(12\%\) of \(50\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} x \amp = 0.12 \cdot 50 \\ \amp = 6 \end{aligned} }\)
Perlia will pay \({\$6.00}\) in tip. In total, she will pay \({\$50.00}+{\$6.00}={\$56.00}\text{.}\)
Perlia will pay \({\$56.00}\) in total (including bill and tip).
Method 3
In the sentence “What is \(12\%\) of \(50\text{,}\)”
  • “what” is the percentage,
  • \(12\%\) is the rate,
  • \(50\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{percentage} = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base}\text{,}\) we do a multiplication to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{percentage } = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base} = 12\% \cdot 50 = 0.12 \cdot 50 = 6 }\)
Perlia will pay \({\$6.00}\) in tip. In total, she will pay \({\$50.00}+{\$6.00}={\$56.00}\text{.}\)
Perlia will pay \({\$56.00}\) in total (including bill and tip).
