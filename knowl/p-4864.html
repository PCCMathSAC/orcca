In first few rows of the table, we see that when the number of weeks \(x\) increases by \(1\text{,}\) the balance \(y\) increases by \(20\text{.}\) The row-to-row rate of change is \(\frac{20\,\text{dollars}}{1\,\text{wk}} = 20\,\frac{\text{dollars}}{\text{wk}}\text{,}\) the slope. In any table for a linear relationship, whenever \(x\) increases by \(1\) unit, \(y\) will increase by the slope.
