Item d.
The two terms are like terms. To combine them, we need to add \(\frac{2}{3}+\frac{5}{6}\text{.}\) For a review of adding fractions with different denominators, see Section A.2. In this case,
\begin{align*} \frac{2}{3}+\frac{5}{6}\amp=\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{2}{2}+\frac{5}{6}\\ \amp=\frac{4}{6}+\frac{5}{6}\\ \amp=\frac{9}{6}=\frac{3}{2} \end{align*}
So \(\frac{2}{3}y+\frac{5}{6}y=\frac{3}{2}y\text{.}\)
