Each cake needs \({1 {\textstyle\frac{4}{5}}}\) cups of flour. To find how many cups of flour are needed to bake \(4\) cakes, we use multiplication:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {1 {\textstyle\frac{4}{5}}} \cdot 4 \amp = \frac{9}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{1} \\ \amp = \frac{36}{5} \\ \amp = {7 {\textstyle\frac{1}{5}}} \end{aligned} }\)
To bake \(4\) cakes, \({7 {\textstyle\frac{1}{5}}}\) cups of flour are needed.
