But now \(x\) is no longer present in the equation. Our effort to remove \(3x\) from the right side coincidentally removed it from the left side too. What value can we substitute in for \(x\) to make \(0=4\) become true? That’s a trick question; you could never make \(0=4\) become true. We say this equation has no solution. Or we say that the equation has an empty solution set. We can write this as \(\emptyset\text{,}\) or \(\{\text{ }\}\text{,}\) which are the symbols for the empty set. (Be careful not to confuse the empty set \(\emptyset\) with the number zero, \(0\text{.}\))
