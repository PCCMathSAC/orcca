  1. A line’s equation in point-slope form looks like \(y=m(x-x_0)+y_0\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \((x_0,y_0)\) is some point we know to be on the line. We first need to find the line’s slope. To find the slope, we need two points on the line. We have been given that \((240,26.4)\) and \((380,31.3)\) are points on the linear model, so we can use the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \text{slope}=\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}} }\)
    First, we mark which number corresponds to which variable in the formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ (240,26.4) \longrightarrow (x_{1},y_{1}) }\)
    \(\displaystyle{ (380,31.3) \longrightarrow (x_{2},y_{2}) }\)
    Now we substitute these numbers into the corresponding variables in the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}\text{slope}\amp =\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\\\amp =\frac{31.3-26.4}{380-240}\\\amp =\frac{4.9}{140}\\\amp =0.035 \end{aligned}}\)
    This line’s slope is \(0.035\text{.}\) This implies you have to pay \({\$0.04}\) per minute that you use the phone.
    So now we have that \(y=0.035(x-x_0)+y_0\text{.}\) We can either use \((x_0,y_0)=(240,26.4)\) or \((x_0,y_0)=(380,31.3)\) to get \({y = 0.035\mathopen{}\left(x-240\right)+26.4\hbox{ or }y = 0.035\mathopen{}\left(x-380\right)+31.3}\text{.}\)
  2. In one month, you spent \(190\) minutes on the phone. To find out the bill, we substitute \(190\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.035(x-240)+26.4\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.035(190-240) +26.4 \\ y \amp = 24.65 \end{aligned} }\)
    If you spent \(190\) minutes over the phone in a month, your bill for that month would be \({\$24.65}\text{.}\)
  3. In one month, you had a cell phone bill of \({\$33.75}\text{.}\) To find how many minutes you spent on the phone that month, we substitute \(33.75\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.035(x-240)+26.4\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.035(x-240) +26.4 \\ 33.75 \amp = 0.035(x-240) +26.4 \\ 7.35 \amp = 0.035(x-240)\\ \frac{7.35}{0.035} \amp = x - 240\\ 210 \amp = x - 240\\ 450 \amp = x\\ 450 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    If your bill was \({\$33.75}\) in one month, you must have spent \(450\) minutes on the phone.
