Explanation 3.7.5.22.1.
A line’s standard form is \(Ax+By=C\text{.}\) We need to move \(x\) and \(y\) terms to the same side of the equals sign.
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} y\amp=-\frac{4}{3}x+2\\ \multiplyleft{3}y\amp=\multiplyleft{3}\left(-\frac{4}{3}x+2\right)\\ 3y\amp=3\left( -\frac{4}{3}x \right)+3(2)\\ 3y\amp=-4x+6\\ 3y\addright{4x}\amp=-4x+6\addright{4x}\\ {4x+3y}\amp=6 \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
