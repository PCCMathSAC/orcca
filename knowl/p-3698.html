  1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] xr \amp = C \\ \frac{xr}{r} \amp = \frac{C}{r} \\ x \amp = {\frac{C}{r}} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({\frac{C}{r}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{C}{r}}\) into the original equation for \(x\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] xr \amp = C \\ \frac{xr}{x} \amp = \frac{C}{x} \\ r \amp = {\frac{C}{x}} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({\frac{C}{x}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{C}{x}}\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
