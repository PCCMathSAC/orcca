Item 1.3.4.75.b.
This problem comes down to looking at the impact of multiplying by \(y\) compared to the impact of adding \(y\text{.}\) Since \(x\gt1\text{,}\) \(x\) is at least \(2\text{.}\) Similarly, \(y\) is at least \(3\text{.}\) Multiplying by \(y\) results in a bigger number than adding \(y\text{.}\)
