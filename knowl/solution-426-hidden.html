Let \(x\) be the number of minutes you talked on the phone that month.
You must pay \({\$0.18}\) per minute of talk time. If you talked \(x\) minutes, you would pay \(0.18x\) dollars.
Now we can write an equation:
\(\displaystyle{ 19+0.18x = 85.6 }\)
Next, we solve for \(x\text{:}\)
\(\begin{aligned} 19 +0.18x \amp = 85.6 \\ 19 +0.18x\mathbf{{}-19} \amp = 85.6\mathbf{{}-19} \\ 0.18x \amp = 66.6 \\ \frac{0.18x}{0.18} \amp = \frac{66.6}{0.18} \\ x \amp = 370 \end{aligned}\)
You spent \({370\ {\rm min}}\) talking on the phone in that month.
