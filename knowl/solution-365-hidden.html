One approach to this problem is to know that horizontal lines have slope \(0\text{.}\)
To be more consistent with how you might approach problems like this that do not have horizontal lines, we would first need to identify two points that the line passes through. It is wise to choose points with integer coordinates. For this problem, we choose \((0,4)\) and \((1,4)\text{.}\)
Next, we sketch a slope triangle and find the rise and run. In the sketch below, the rise is \(0\) and the run is \(1\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned}\text{slope}\amp =\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}\\ \amp =\frac{0}{1}\\\amp =0 \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
This line’s slope is \(0\text{.}\)
