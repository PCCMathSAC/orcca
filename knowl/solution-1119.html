Explanation A.6.4.22.1.

To be an irrational number, you simply have to not be a rational number. A rational number is a number that can be written as a fraction with whole numbers for the numerator and denominator, or with a terminating decimal expression, or with a repeating decimal expression. (Actually, these descriptions are a little redundant.) Sometimes rational numbers are explicitly written in one of these ways, and sometimes they are hidden. For example \(\sqrt{0.16}\) is a rational number, since \(\sqrt{0.16}=0.4\text{.}\) The irrational numbers here are are FH.
