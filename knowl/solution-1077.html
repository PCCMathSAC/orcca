Explanation A.4.3.72.1.

To calculate the percentage increase/decrease, first we find the amount of decrease/decrease, and then we find this increase/decrease is what percent of the original value.
In this problem, the stock’s price was \({\$0.81}\) on 11/1, and the price changed to \({\$0.49}\) on 11/5. Their difference was \({\$0.49}-{\$0.81}={-\$0.32}\text{,}\) so the amount of change was \({\$0.32}\text{.}\)
Next, we need to find: \({\$0.32}\) is what percent of the original value, \({\$0.81}\text{.}\)
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(0.32\) is \(x\%\) of \(0.81\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(0.32\) out of \(0.81\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{0.32}{0.81} \\ 0.81x \amp = 0.32 \cdot 100 \\ 0.81x \amp = 32 \\ \frac{0.81x}{0.81} \amp = \frac{32}{0.81} \\ x \amp \approx 0.395061728395062 \\ x \amp \approx 39.51 \end{aligned} }\)
From 11/1 to 11/5, the percentage change in the stock’s value was approximately \(39.51\%\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Let the change \(0.32\) be \(x\) (as a percent) of \(0.81\text{.}\) That means:
\(\begin{aligned} 0.32 \amp = x \cdot 0.81 \\ \frac{0.32}{0.81} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 0.81}{0.81} \\ 0.395061728395062 \amp \approx x \\ 0.3951 \amp \approx x \\ 39.51\% \amp \approx x \\ \end{aligned}\)
From 11/1 to 11/5, the percentage change in the stock’s value was approximately \(39.51\%\text{.}\)
Method 3
We first divide the “new number” by the “original number”:
\(\displaystyle{ \frac{0.49}{0.81} \approx 0.604938271604938 \approx 139.51\% }\)
So the new number is approximately \(139.51\%\) of the original number, implying the percentage change was approximately \(139.51\% - 100\% = 39.51\%\text{.}\)
From 11/1 to 11/5, the percentage change in the stock’s value was approximately \(39.51\%\text{.}\)
