Two approaches are shown below.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} -48 \amp = -8(q -3) \amp -48 \amp = -8(q -3)\\ -48 \amp = -8(q +(-3)) \amp \frac{-48}{-8} \amp = \frac{-8(q -3)}{-8}\\ -48 \amp = -8q +(-8) \cdot (-3) \amp 6 \amp = q -3\\ -48 \amp = -8q +24 \amp 6\mathbf{{}+3} \amp = q -3\mathbf{{}+3}\\ -48 \mathbf{{}-24} \amp = -8q +24\mathbf{{}-24} \amp 9 \amp = q\\ -72 \amp = -8q \amp q \amp = 9\\ \frac{-72}{-8} \amp = \frac{-8q}{-8} \\ 9 \amp = q\\ q \amp = 9 \end{aligned} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(9\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(q\text{,}\) some report \(q = 9\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{9\}\text{,}\) or that \(q\in\left\{9\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(9\) in for \(q\) in the original equation \(-48=-8\mathopen{}\left(q-3\right)\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
