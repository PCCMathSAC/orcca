There are at least two methods to reduce the fraction \(\displaystyle{ \frac{7}{28} }\text{.}\)
Method 1:We find a number that divides into both the numerator \(7\text{,}\) and the denominator \(28\text{.}\)
Check the first few prime numbers one by one: \(2, 3, 5, 7, \ldots\)
In this case, \(7\) goes into both the numerator and denominator of \(\displaystyle{ \frac{7}{28} }\text{.}\) We divide \(7\) into both numbers, and we have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} \frac{7}{28} \amp = \frac{7 \div 7}{28\div 7}\\ \amp = \frac{1}{4}\end{aligned} }\)
Next, check again whether any prime number divides into both the numerator and denominator. We need to keep trying until no prime numbers divide into both numbers.
In this case, \(\displaystyle{\frac{1}{4}}\) is the final answer.
Method 2:
A second method to reduce fraction is to prime factor both the numerator and the denominator, and then cancel out factors in pairs: one from the numerator and one from the denominator.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{7}{28} \amp = \frac{1 \cdot 7}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 7} \\ \amp =\frac{1}{2 \cdot 2}\\ \amp = \frac{1}{4} \end{aligned} }\)
Notice that when the numerator’s only prime factor, \(7\text{,}\) is canceled, we have to leave a \(1\) in the numerator.
