Explanation 2.5.3.24.1.
  1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] y+r \amp = b \\ y+r\mathbf{{}-r} \amp = b\mathbf{{}-r} \\ y \amp = {b-r} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({b-r}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({b-r}\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] y+r \amp = b \\ y+r\mathbf{{}-y} \amp = b\mathbf{{}-y} \\ r \amp = {b-y} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({b-y}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({b-y}\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
