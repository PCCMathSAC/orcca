This problem comes down to looking at the impact of multiplying by \(y\) compared to the impact of adding \(y\text{.}\) Since \(0 \lt y \lt 1\text{,}\) this means that \(y\) is a fraction between \(0\) and \(1\text{.}\) Thus, multiplying by \(y\) results in a smaller number, whereas adding \(y\) results in a bigger number.
