We could do some side work to calculate the \(y\)-intercept, but let’s try something else. Here are some things we know:
  1. The slope equation is \(m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\text{.}\)
  2. The slope is \(m=2.865\,\frac{\text{million people}}{\text{year}}\text{,}\) or \(m=\frac{2.865\,\text{million people}}{1\,\text{year}}\text{.}\)
  3. One point on the line is \((1990,253)\text{,}\) because in 1990, the population was 253 million.
