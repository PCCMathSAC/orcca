Item 3.6.5.63.b.
In the year \(2011\text{,}\) \(x\) was \(11\text{.}\) To find the amount remaining in the fund, we substitute \(11\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-29000(x-4)+540000\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -29000(11-4) +540000 \\ y \amp = 337000 \end{aligned} }\)
So in the year \(2011\text{,}\) the fund had \({\$337{,}000}\) remaining in it.
