Explanation A.4.3.66.1.

To calculate the percentage increase/decrease, first we find the amount of increase/decrease by doing a simple subtraction calculation, and then we find the percentage increase/decrease.
In this problem, the amount of decrease is \(620-616=4\text{.}\)
Since the city’s population was initially \(620\text{,}\) we need to ask: \(4\) is what percent of \(620\text{?}\)
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(4\) is \(x\%\) of \(620\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(4\) out of \(620\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{4}{620} \\ 620x \amp = 4 \cdot 100 \\ 620x \amp = 400 \\ \frac{620x}{620} \amp = \frac{400}{620} \\ x \amp \approx 0.645161290322581 \\ x \amp \approx 0.65 \end{aligned} }\)
The percentage decrease of the town’s population was approximately \(0.65\%\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Let the decrease \(4\) be \(x\) (as a percent) of \(620\text{.}\) That means:
\(\begin{aligned} 4 \amp = x \cdot 620 \\ \frac{4}{620} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 620}{620} \\ 0.00645161290322581 \amp \approx x\\ x \amp \approx 0.65\% \\ \end{aligned}\)
The percentage decrease of the town’s population was approximately \(0.65\%\text{.}\)
Method 3
We first divide the “new number” by the “original number”:
\(\displaystyle{ \frac{616}{620} \approx 0.993548387096774 \approx 99.35\% }\)
So the new number is \(99.35\%\) of the original number, implying the percentage decrease is \(100\% - 99.35\% = 0.65\%\text{.}\)
The percentage decrease of the town’s population was approximately \(0.65\%\text{.}\)
