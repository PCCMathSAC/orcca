Item 3.7.11.a.
To find the \(x\)-intercept:
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} 2x+4.3y\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ 2x+4.3(\substitute{0})\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ 2x\amp=\frac{1000}{99}\\ x\amp=\frac{500}{99} \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
So the \(x\)-intercept is at \(\left(\frac{500}{99},0\right)\text{.}\)
