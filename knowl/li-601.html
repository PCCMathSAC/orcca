Item 3.5.7.71.c.
To find when the gas will be all gone, we need to find when there are \(0\) grams of gas left. We can substitute \(0\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=-9.9x+465.3\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -9.9x+465.3 \\ 0 \amp = -9.9x +465.3 \\ 0\mathbf{{}-465.3} \amp = -9.9x +465.3\mathbf{{}-465.3}\\ -465.3 \amp = -9.9x \\ \frac{-465.3}{-9.9} \amp = \frac{-9.9x}{-9.9} \\ 47 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(47\) minutes, the gas will be all gone.
