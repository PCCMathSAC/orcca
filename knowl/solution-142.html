Explanation 2.3.5.48.1.
Let \(x\) represent how many deer are in the Jewell Meadow Area. We set up a proportional equation:
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \frac{\text{tagged deer}}{\text{total deer}} \amp= \frac{\text{tagged deer}}{\text{total deer}}\\ \frac{9}{63} \amp= \frac{80}{x}\\ \frac{9}{63} \multiplyright{63\cdot x} \amp= \frac{80}{x}\multiplyright{63\cdot x}\\ 9 \cdot x \amp= 80\cdot 63\\ \divideunder{9 \cdot x}{9} \amp= \divideunder{80\cdot 63}{9}\\ x \amp= 560 \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
There are approximately \(560\) deer in the Jewell Meadow Area.
