Item 3.10.9.26.b.
After \(33\) minutes, we can find the mass of the remaining gas if we substitute \(33\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-2.5x+100\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -2.5x+100 \\ y \amp = -2.5 \cdot 33 +100 \\ y \amp = -82.5+100 \\ y \amp = 17.5 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(33\) minutes, there are \(17.5\) grams of gas remaining.
