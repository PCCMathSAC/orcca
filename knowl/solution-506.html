Explanation 3.6.5.63.1.
  1. A line’s equation in point-slope form looks like \(y=m(x-x_0)+y_0\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \((x_0,y_0)\) is some point we know to be on the line. We first need to find the line’s slope. To find the slope, we need two points on the line. We have been given that \((4,540000)\) and \((5,511000)\) are points on the linear model, so we can use the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \text{slope}=\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}} }\)
    First, we mark which number corresponds to which variable in the formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ (4,540000) \longrightarrow (x_{1},y_{1}) }\)
    \(\displaystyle{ (5,511000) \longrightarrow (x_{2},y_{2}) }\)
    Now we substitute these numbers into the corresponding variables in the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}\text{slope}\amp =\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\\\amp =\frac{511000-540000}{5-4}\\\amp =\frac{-29000}{1}\\\amp =-29000 \end{aligned}}\)
    This line’s slope is \(-29000\text{.}\) This implies that the company is spending \({\$29{,}000}\) per year on perks for its employees.
    So now we have that \(y=-29000(x-x_0)+y_0\text{.}\) We can either use \((x_0,y_0)=(4,540000)\) or \((x_0,y_0)=(5,511000)\) to get \({y = -29000\mathopen{}\left(x-4\right)+540000\hbox{ or }y = -29000\mathopen{}\left(x-5\right)+511000}\text{.}\)
  2. In the year \(2011\text{,}\) \(x\) was \(11\text{.}\) To find the amount remaining in the fund, we substitute \(11\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-29000(x-4)+540000\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -29000(11-4) +540000 \\ y \amp = 337000 \end{aligned} }\)
    So in the year \(2011\text{,}\) the fund had \({\$337{,}000}\) remaining in it.
  3. The fund will be empty when it has \({\$0}\) left in it. That is, \(y\) will equal \(0\text{.}\) To find how many years until this happens, we substitute \(0\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=-29000(x-4)+540000\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -29000(x-4) +540000 \\ 0 \amp = -29000(x-4) +540000 \\ -540000 \amp = -29000(x-4)\\ \frac{-540000}{-29000} \amp = x - 4\\ 18.6206896551724 \amp = x - 4\\ 22.6206896551724 \amp = x\\ 22.62 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    Approximately \(22.62\) years after 2000, in the year 2022, the fund will be depleted.
