A line’s equation in point-slope form looks like \(y=m(x-x_0)+y_0\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \((x_0,y_0)\) is some point we know to be on the line. We first need to find the line’s slope. We have been told that the account decreases by \({\$50{,}000}\) each year, so the slope of the linear model is \(-50000\) (dollars per year).
