We substitute in each ordered pair’s \(x\)- and \(y\)-values into the equation \(y=\frac{4}{5} x-5\text{,}\) and see whether the resulting equation is true.
In two cases, \((-25,-25)\) and \((5,-1)\text{,}\) we have true equations:
\(\begin{aligned} -25 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}\frac{4}{5}(-25)-5 \amp -1 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}\frac{4}{5}(5)-5\\ -25 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-20-5 \amp -1 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}4-5\\ -25 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-25 \amp -1 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-1\\ \end{aligned}\)
So \((-25,-25)\) and \((5,-1)\) are solutions to the given equation.
In the other two cases, \((-5,-8)\) and \((0,0)\text{,}\) we have false equations:
\(\begin{aligned} -8 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}\frac{4}{5}(-5)-5 \amp 0 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}\frac{4}{5}(0)-5\\ -8 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-4-5 \amp 0 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}0-5\\ -8 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-9 \amp 0 \amp \stackrel{?}{=}-5\\ \end{aligned}\)
So \((-5,-8)\) and \((0,0)\) are not solutions.
