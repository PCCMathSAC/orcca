We can see a pattern here that may help with future percent applications.
\begin{equation} (\text{initial value}) \pm (\text{percent as decimal})\cdot(\text{initial value}) = (\text{final value})\tag{1.8.2} \end{equation}
In Jakobi’s sutuation, his salary rose, so the \(\pm\) symbols would be a \(+\text{.}\) We can imagine other applications (like a discount at a clothing store) where the initial value is reduced, and then the \(\pm\) symbol would be a \(-\text{.}\)
