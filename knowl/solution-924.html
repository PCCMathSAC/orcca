Explanation A.2.7.83.1.

By the pie chart, the school has \({{\frac{3}{8}}}\) white students, and \({{\frac{1}{12}}}\) black students.
We will use addition to find the total portion of white and black students at this school.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{{}=}{{\frac{3}{8}}}+{{\frac{1}{12}}} \\ \amp = \frac{3 \cdot 3}{8 \cdot 3} + \frac{1 \cdot 3}{12 \cdot 3} \\ \amp = \frac{9}{24} + \frac{2}{24} \\ \amp = \frac{9+2}{24} \\ \amp = \frac{11}{24} \\ \end{aligned} }\)
Together, white and black students make up \({{\frac{11}{24}}}\) of the school’s population.
