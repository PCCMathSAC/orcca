A chemist has a bottle with 1.2 L of water mixed with 0.3 L of gasoline. At this time, they have forgotten the density of water (in g⁄L) and the denisty of gasoline (also in g⁄L) so they use \(w\) and \(g\) as variables for these densities. This means \(1.2w+0.3g\) is the total mass of this mixture, in grams.
