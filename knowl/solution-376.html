Do your best to identify two points on the line. With this line, it seems \((2006,19000)\) and \((2014,21000)\) are two such points.
\begin{equation*} \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}=\frac{2000\,\text{diagnoses}}{8\,\text{yr}}=250\,\frac{\text{diagnoses}}{\text{yr}} \end{equation*}
So the slope is about \(250\) diagnoses per year. This is only an estimate since we are not all certain the two points we chose are actually on the line.
