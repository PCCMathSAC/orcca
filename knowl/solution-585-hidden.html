To find a line’s slope, we can use the slope formula:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{slope}=\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}} }\)
For line \(m\text{,}\) we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} \text{slope}\amp =\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\\ \amp =\frac{-5-(-11)}{1-3}\\ \amp =\frac{6}{-2}=-3 \end{aligned}}\)
For line \(n\text{,}\) we have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}\text{slope}\amp =\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\\ \amp =\frac{9-(-1)}{1-(-1)}\\ \amp =\frac{10}{2}\\ \amp =5 \end{aligned}}\)
Since \(-3 \neq 5\text{,}\) these two lines are not parallel.
Since \(-3 \cdot 5 \neq -1\text{,}\) these two lines are not perpendicular.
The correct answer is these two lines are neither parallel nor perpendicular.
