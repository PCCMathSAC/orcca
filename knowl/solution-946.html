Explanation A.2.7.109.1.

Since the fractions don’t have the same denominator, we have to make them have like denominators. Multiply the first fraction by \(\frac{5}{5}\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} {\frac{9}{a}-\frac{7}{5a}} \amp = {\frac{9}{a}}\cdot \frac{5}{5} - {\frac{7}{5a}}\\ \amp = \frac{45}{5a} - {\frac{7}{5a}}\\ \amp = {\frac{38}{5a}} \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
