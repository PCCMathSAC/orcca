Item 3.5.7.66.c.
The fund will be empty when it has \({\$0}\) left in it. That is, \(y\) will equal \(0\text{.}\) To find how many years until this happens, we substitute \(0\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=-23000x+896000\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -23000x+896000 \\ 0 \amp = -23000x +896000 \\ 0\mathbf{{}-896000} \amp = -23000x +896000\mathbf{{}-896000} \\ -896000 \amp = -23000x \\ \frac{-896000}{-23000} \amp = \frac{-23000x}{-23000} \\ 38.96 \amp \approx x \end{aligned} }\)
Approximately \(38.96\) years after 2000, in the year 2038, the fund will be depleted.
