Explanation A.2.7.97.1.

To find the total length in inches, we add the two lengths together.
\(\begin{aligned} {36 {\textstyle\frac{3}{4}}}+{57 {\textstyle\frac{1}{16}}} \amp = 36+\frac{3}{4}+57+\frac{1}{16}\\ \amp = 93+\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{16}\\ \amp = 93+{{\frac{13}{16}}}\\ \amp = {93 {\textstyle\frac{13}{16}}} \end{aligned}\)
So the total length is \({93 {\textstyle\frac{13}{16}}}\) inches.
