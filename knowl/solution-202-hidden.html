1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] 9y \amp = 36 \\ \frac{9y}{9} \amp = \frac{36}{9} \\ y \amp = 4 \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \(4\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(4\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] Br \amp = a \\ \frac{Br}{B} \amp = \frac{a}{B} \\ r \amp = {\frac{a}{B}} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({\frac{a}{B}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{a}{B}}\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
