Explanation 3.5.7.64.1.
Assume Lisa can purchase \(x\) Pokemon cards.
Each card costs \({\$1.05}\text{,}\) so \(x\) cards will cost \(1.05x\) dollars.
Currently, Lisa has \({\$88}\) in his piggy bank. After spending \(1.05x\) dollars, he would have \(88-1.05x\) dollars left.
Lisa will keep \({\$72.25}\) unspent. Now we can write an equation:
\(\displaystyle{ 88-1.05x = 72.25 }\)
Next, we solve for \(x\text{:}\)
\(\begin{aligned} 88-1.05x \amp = 72.25 \\ 88-1.05x \mathbf{{}-88} \amp = 72.25 \mathbf{{}-88} \\ -1.05x \amp = -15.75 \\ \frac{-1.05x}{-1.05} \amp = \frac{-15.75}{-1.05} \\ x \amp = 15 \end{aligned}\)
Lisa can purchase at most \(15\) Pokemon cards.
