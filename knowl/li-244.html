Item 2.5.3.18.a.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{t}{9} \amp = 8 \\ 9 \cdot \frac{t}{9} \amp = 9 \cdot 8 \\ t \amp = 72 \\ \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \(72\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(72\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
