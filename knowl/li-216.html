Item 2.5.3.11.a.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] t - 5 \amp = -3 \\ t - 5\mathbf{{}+5} \amp = -3\mathbf{{}+5} \\ t \amp = 2 \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \(2\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(2\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
