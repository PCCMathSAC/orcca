To calculate the percentage increase/decrease, first we find the amount of increase/decrease by doing a simple subtraction calculation, and then we find the percentage increase/decrease.
In this problem, the amount of decrease is \(590-588=2\text{.}\)
Since the city’s population was initially \(590\text{,}\) we need to ask: \(2\) is what percent of \(590\text{?}\)
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(2\) is \(x\%\) of \(590\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(2\) out of \(590\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{2}{590} \\ 590x \amp = 2 \cdot 100 \\ 590x \amp = 200 \\ \frac{590x}{590} \amp = \frac{200}{590} \\ x \amp \approx 0.338983050847458 \\ x \amp \approx 0.34 \end{aligned} }\)
The percentage decrease of the town’s population was approximately \(0.34\%\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Let the decrease \(2\) be \(x\) (as a percent) of \(590\text{.}\) That means:
\(\begin{aligned} 2 \amp = x \cdot 590 \\ \frac{2}{590} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 590}{590} \\ 0.00338983050847458 \amp \approx x\\ x \amp \approx 0.34\% \\ \end{aligned}\)
The percentage decrease of the town’s population was approximately \(0.34\%\text{.}\)
Method 3
We first divide the “new number” by the “original number”:
\(\displaystyle{ \frac{588}{590} \approx 0.996610169491525 \approx 99.66\% }\)
So the new number is \(99.66\%\) of the original number, implying the percentage decrease is \(100\% - 99.66\% = 0.34\%\text{.}\)
The percentage decrease of the town’s population was approximately \(0.34\%\text{.}\)
