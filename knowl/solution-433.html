Explanation 3.5.7.67.1.
  1. A line’s equation in slope-intercept form looks like \(y=mx+b\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \(y\) is the \(y\)-coordinate of the \(y\)-intercept. Since the tree grows at a rate of \(0.14\) feet per month, \(0.14\) is the slope of the linear model.
    So now we have that \(y=0.14x+b\text{.}\) The next step is to find the value of \(b\text{.}\) There are at least two ways.
    One way is to substitute a known point into \(y=0.14x+b\text{.}\) We use the point \((11,15.64)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.14x + b \\ 15.64 \amp = 0.14 \cdot 11 + b \\ 15.64 \amp = 1.54 + b \\ 15.64\mathbf{{}-1.54} \amp = 1.54 + b\mathbf{{}-1.54} \\ 14.1 \amp = b\\ b \amp = 14.1 \end{aligned}\)
    This line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=0.14x+14.1\text{.}\)
    Another way is to use the line’s point-slope equation \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\text{.}\) Again, we use the point \((11,15.64)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y-y_{1} \amp = m(x-x_{1}) \\ y-15.64 \amp = 0.14(x-11) \\ y-15.64 \amp = 0.14x+0.14 \cdot (-11) \\ y-15.64 \amp = 0.14x - 1.54 \\ y-15.64\mathbf{{}+15.64} \amp = 0.14x - 1.54\mathbf{{}+15.64} \\ y \amp = 0.14x+14.1 \end{aligned}\)
    This is the second way to find \(b\text{,}\) and thus the line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=0.14x+14.1\text{.}\)
  2. After \(29\) months, we can find the height of the tree if we substitute \(29\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.14x+14.1\text{.}\) We have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.14x+14.1 \\ y \amp = 0.14 \cdot 29 +14.1 \\ y \amp = 4.06+14.1 \\ y \amp = 18.16 \end{aligned} }\)
    So after \(29\) months, the tree is \(\) feet tall.
  3. To find when the tree was \(\) feet tall, we can substitute \(21.1\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.14x+14.1\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.14x+14.1 \\ 21.1 \amp = 0.14x +14.1 \\ 21.1\mathbf{{}-14.1} \amp = 0.14x +14.1\mathbf{{}-14.1}\\ 7 \amp = 0.14x \\ \frac{7}{0.14} \amp = \frac{0.14x}{0.14} \\ 50 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    So the tree was \(21.1\) feet tall after \(50\) months.
