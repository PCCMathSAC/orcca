Explanation A.4.3.51.1.

Jessica’s pay is made up of base pay and commission. In that month, Jessica earned a total of \({\$4{,}113.68}\text{,}\) with \({\$1{,}100.00}\) of base pay. This implies Jessica earned \({\$4{,}113.68}-{\$1{,}100.00}={\$3{,}013.68}\) in commission, out of \({\$69{,}600.00}\) in sales.
Now the problem can be boiled down to this question: \(3013.68\) is what percent of \(69600\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(3013.68\) is \(x\%\) of \(69600\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(3013.68\) out of \(69600\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{3013.68}{69600} \\ 69600x \amp = 3013.68 \cdot 100 \\ 69600x \amp = 301368 \\ \frac{69600x}{69600} \amp = \frac{301368}{69600} \\ x \amp = 4.33 \end{aligned} }\)
Jessica earned \(4.33\%\) in commission.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(3013.68\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(69600\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 3013.68 \amp = x \cdot 69600 \\ \frac{3013.68}{69600} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 69600}{69600} \\ 0.0433 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 4.33\% \end{aligned} }\)
Jessica earned \(4.33\%\) in commission.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(3013.68\) is what percent of \(69600\text{,}\)”
  • \(3013.68\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(69600\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{3013.68}{69600} = 0.0433 = 4.33\% }\)
Jessica earned \(4.33\%\) in commission.
