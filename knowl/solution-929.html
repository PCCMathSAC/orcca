Explanation A.2.7.88.1.

When we use the word “of” in situations like “\({{\frac{1}{3}}}\) of \({\$12{,}900}\text{,}\)” we can translate “of” into the multiplication symbol.
In this problem, to find \({{\frac{1}{3}}}\) of \({\$12{,}900}\text{,}\) we do:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {{\frac{1}{3}}} \cdot 12900 \amp = {{\frac{1}{3}}} \cdot \frac{12900}{1} \\ \amp = \frac{1}{1} \cdot \frac{4300}{1} \\ \amp = 4300 \end{aligned} }\)
The car is worth \({\$4{,}300}\) now.
