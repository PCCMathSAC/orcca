Item 3.5.7.68.b.
After \(37\) minutes, we can find the mass of the remaining gas if we substitute \(37\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-1.4x+60.2\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -1.4x+60.2 \\ y \amp = -1.4 \cdot 37 +60.2 \\ y \amp = -51.8+60.2 \\ y \amp = 8.4 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(37\) minutes, there are \(8.4\) grams of gas remaining.
