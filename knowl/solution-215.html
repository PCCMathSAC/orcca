Explanation 2.5.3.28.1.
Solution of Part a):
\(\begin{aligned}[t] x \amp = yp+m \\ x\mathbf{{}-m}\amp = yp +m\mathbf{{}-m} \\ x-m \amp = yp \\ \frac{x-m}{y} \amp = \frac{yp}{y} \\ {\frac{x-m}{y}} \amp = p\\ p \amp = {\frac{x-m}{y}} \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({\frac{x-m}{y}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{x-m}{y}}\) into the original equation for \(p\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
Solution of Part b):
\(\begin{aligned}[t] x \amp = yp +m \\ x\mathbf{{}-m}\amp = yp +m\mathbf{{}-m} \\ x-m \amp = yp \\ \frac{x-m}{p} \amp = \frac{yp}{p} \\ {\frac{x-m}{p}} \amp = y\\ y \amp = {\frac{x-m}{p}} \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({\frac{x-m}{p}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{x-m}{p}}\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
