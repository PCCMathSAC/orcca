Example 1.2.7.

In the expressions below, look for like terms and then simplify where possible by adding or subtracting.
  1. \(\displaystyle 5\,\text{in}+20\,\text{in}\)
  2. \(\displaystyle 16\,\text{ft}^2+4\,\text{ft}\)
  3. \(\displaystyle 2\,\apple+5\,\apple\)
  4. \(\displaystyle 5\,\text{min}+12\,\text{ft}\)
  5. \(\displaystyle 5\,\dog-2\,\cat\)
  6. \(\displaystyle 20\,\text{m}-6\,\text{m}\)
Explanation.
We can combine terms with the same units, but we cannot combine terms with distinct units such as minutes and feet.
  1. \(\displaystyle 5\,\text{in}+20\,\text{in}=25\,\text{in}\)
  2. \(16\,\text{ft}^2+4\,\text{ft}\) cannot be simplified
  3. \(\displaystyle 2\,\apple+5\,\apple=7\,\apple\)
  4. \(5\,\text{min}+12\,\text{ft}\) cannot be simplified
  5. \(5\,\dog-2\,\cat\) cannot be simplified
  6. \(\displaystyle 20\,\text{m}-6\,\text{m}=14\,\text{m}\)
