Explanation A.2.7.17.1.

To multiply two fractions, simply multiply both numerators and both denominators. We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] \frac{1}{7} \cdot \frac{5}{8} \amp = \frac{1 \cdot 5}{7 \cdot 8} \\ \amp = \frac{5}{56} \end{aligned} }\)
The answer to this question is \(\displaystyle{\frac{5}{56}}\text{.}\)
