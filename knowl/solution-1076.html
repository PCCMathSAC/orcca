Explanation A.4.3.71.1.

To calculate the percentage increase/decrease, first we find the amount of decrease/decrease, and then we find this increase/decrease is what percent of the original value.
In this problem, the stock’s price was \({\$1.39}\) on 11/1, and the price changed to \({\$1.68}\) on 11/5. Their difference was \({\$1.68}-{\$1.39}={\$0.29}\text{,}\) so the amount of change was \({\$0.29}\text{.}\)
Next, we need to find: \({\$0.29}\) is what percent of the original value, \({\$1.39}\text{.}\)
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(0.29\) is \(x\%\) of \(1.39\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(0.29\) out of \(1.39\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{0.29}{1.39} \\ 1.39x \amp = 0.29 \cdot 100 \\ 1.39x \amp = 29 \\ \frac{1.39x}{1.39} \amp = \frac{29}{1.39} \\ x \amp \approx 0.20863309352518 \\ x \amp \approx 20.86 \end{aligned} }\)
From 11/1 to 11/5, the percentage change in the stock’s value was approximately \(20.86\%\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Let the change \(0.29\) be \(x\) (as a percent) of \(1.39\text{.}\) That means:
\(\begin{aligned} 0.29 \amp = x \cdot 1.39 \\ \frac{0.29}{1.39} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 1.39}{1.39} \\ 0.20863309352518 \amp \approx x \\ 0.2086 \amp \approx x \\ 20.86\% \amp \approx x \\ \end{aligned}\)
From 11/1 to 11/5, the percentage change in the stock’s value was approximately \(20.86\%\text{.}\)
Method 3
We first divide the “new number” by the “original number”:
\(\displaystyle{ \frac{1.68}{1.39} \approx 1.20863309352518 \approx 120.86\% }\)
So the new number is approximately \(120.86\%\) of the original number, implying the percentage change was approximately \(120.86\% - 100\% = 20.86\%\text{.}\)
From 11/1 to 11/5, the percentage change in the stock’s value was approximately \(20.86\%\text{.}\)
