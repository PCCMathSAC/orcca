There is no operation symbols between \(0.072\) and \(0.084\text{,}\) which implies multiplication.
Next, since “negative times negative is positive,” the problem becomes:
\(\displaystyle{ (-0.072)(-0.084) = 0.072 \cdot 0.084 }\)
We know the answer must be positive.
It’s given that \(72\cdot84=6048\text{.}\) We need to calculate \(0.072 \cdot 0.084\text{.}\)
Notice that \(0.072=\frac{72}{1000}\) and \(0.084=\frac{84}{1000}\text{,}\) so we can calculate \(0.072 \cdot 0.084\) in this way:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{{}=} 0.072 \cdot 0.084 \\ \amp = \frac{72}{1000} \cdot \frac{84}{1000} \\ \amp = \frac{72\cdot84}{1000\cdot1000} \\ \amp = \frac{6048}{1000000} \\ \amp = 0.006048 \end{aligned} }\)
Once we understand the math, we can use this shortcut.
  1. To change \(72\) to \(0.072\text{,}\) we moved the decimal point to the left by \(3\) place(s).
  2. To change \(84\) to \(0.084\text{,}\) we moved the decimal point to the left by \(3\) place(s).
  3. We move the decimal point of \(6048\) to the left by \(3+3=6\) places to get \(0.072\cdot0.084=0.006048\text{.}\)
\(\displaystyle{ (-0.072)(-0.084) = 0.006048 }\)
