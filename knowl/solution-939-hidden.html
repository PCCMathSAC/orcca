To find the total length in inches, we add the two lengths together.
\(\begin{aligned} {53 {\textstyle\frac{3}{4}}}+{46 {\textstyle\frac{3}{4}}} \amp = 53+\frac{3}{4}+46+\frac{3}{4}\\ \amp = 99+\frac{3}{4}+\frac{3}{4}\\ \amp = 99+{1 {\textstyle\frac{1}{2}}}\\ \amp = {100 {\textstyle\frac{1}{2}}} \end{aligned}\)
So the total length is \({100 {\textstyle\frac{1}{2}}}\) inches.
