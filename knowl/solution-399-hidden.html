When an equation of a line is written in the form \(y=mx+b\text{,}\) it is said to be in slope-intercept form. In this form, \(m\) is the line’s slope, and \(b\) is the coordinate on the \(y\)-axis where the line intercepts the \(y\)-axis.
In this problem, the line’s equation is given as \(\displaystyle{ y= -\frac{4x}{7} +4 }\text{.}\) We can think of the first term as \(\displaystyle{-\frac{4}{7}x }\text{.}\) This is because:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}-\frac{4x}{7} \amp = -\frac{4}{7} \cdot \frac{x}{1}\\\amp = -\frac{4}{7}x \end{aligned}}\)
Thus the line’s slope is \(\displaystyle{-\frac{4}{7}}\text{,}\) and its \(y\)-intercept has coordinates \((0,4)\text{.}\)
