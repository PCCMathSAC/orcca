Since the fractions don’t have the same denominator, we have to make them have like denominators. Multiply the first fraction by \(\frac{2}{2}\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} {\frac{8}{a}+\frac{1}{2a}} \amp = {\frac{8}{a}}\cdot \frac{2}{2} + {\frac{1}{2a}}\\ \amp = \frac{16}{2a} + {\frac{1}{2a}}\\ \amp = {\frac{17}{2a}} \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
