Explanation A.4.3.67.1.

Question 1
Your original salary was \({\$32{,}000}\) per year. The amount of cut was \(4\%\) of \({\$32{,}000}\text{.}\) There are two methods to find this amount.
We can use proportion. Let \(x\) dollars be the amount of decrease, then we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{32000} \amp = \frac{4}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 32000\cdot4 \\ 100x \amp = 128000 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{128000}{100} \\ x \amp = 1280 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of increase:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.04 \cdot {\$32{,}000} = {\$1{,}280} }\)
After the cut, your salary became \({\$32{,}000}-{\$1{,}280} = {\$30{,}720}\) dollars per year.
Question 2
Next, the amount of pay raise was \(4\%\) of \({\$30{,}720}\text{.}\) Notice that it’s incorrect to find \(4\%\) of \({\$32{,}000}\text{,}\) because the annual salary has changed from \({\$32{,}000}\) to \({\$30{,}720}\text{.}\)
We will first use proportion to find the amount of pay cut. Assume the amount of pay cut was \(x\) dollars. We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{30720} \amp = \frac{4}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 30720\cdot4 \\ 100x \amp = 122880 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{122880}{100} \\ x \amp = 1228.8 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of pay cut:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.04 \cdot {\$30{,}720} = {\$1{,}228.80} }\)
After the raise, your salary became \({\$30{,}720}+{\$1{,}228.80} = {\$31{,}948.80}\) per year.
After a cut and a raise of the same rate, your salary decreased! This is because the rate of cut was based on a bigger salary, while the rate of raise was based on a smaller salary (after the cut).
