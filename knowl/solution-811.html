Explanation A.1.7.46.1.

When doing decimal subtractions, make sure to line up the decimal points. For the integer \(12\text{,}\) the decimal point is at the end of the number, like in \(12.00\)
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{-}12.00 \\ \amp \underline{-\phantom{1}4.01\phantom{ad}} \\ \amp \phantom{-}7.99 \end{aligned} }\)
