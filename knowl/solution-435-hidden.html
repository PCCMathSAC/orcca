1. A line’s equation in slope-intercept form looks like \(y=mx+b\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \(y\) is the \(y\)-coordinate of the \(y\)-intercept. We first need to find the line’s slope. To find the slope, we need two points on the line. We have been given that \((2,812000)\) and \((5,713000)\) are points on the linear model, so we can use the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \text{slope}=\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}} }\)
    First, we mark which number corresponds to which variable in the formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ (2,812000) \longrightarrow (x_{1},y_{1}) }\)
    \(\displaystyle{ (5,713000) \longrightarrow (x_{2},y_{2}) }\)
    Now we substitute these numbers into the corresponding variables in the slope formula:
    \(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}\text{slope}\amp =\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}\\\amp =\frac{713000-812000}{5-2}\\\amp =\frac{-99000}{3}\\\amp =-33000 \end{aligned}}\)
    This line’s slope is \(-33000\text{.}\) This implies that the company is spending \({\$33{,}000}\) per year on perks for its employees.
    So now we have that \(y=-33000x+b\text{.}\) The next step is to find the value of \(b\text{.}\) There are at least two ways.
    One way is to substitute one of the given points into \(y=-33000x+b\text{.}\) We choose to use \((2,812000)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y \amp = -33000x + b \\ 812000 \amp = -33000 \cdot 2 + b \\ 812000 \amp = -66000 + b \\ 812000\mathbf{{} + 66000} \amp = -66000 + b\mathbf{{} + 66000} \\ 878000 \amp = b\\ b \amp = 878000 \end{aligned}\)
    This line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=-33000x+878000\text{.}\)
    Another way is to use the line’s point-slope equation \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\text{.}\) Again, we choose to use the point \((2,812000)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y-y_{1} \amp = m(x-x_{1}) \\ y-812000 \amp = -33000(x-2) \\ y-812000 \amp = -33000x - 33000 \cdot (-2) \\ y-812000 \amp = -33000x+66000 \\ y-812000\mathbf{{}+812000} \amp = -33000x+66000\mathbf{{}+812000} \\ y \amp = -33000x+878000 \end{aligned}\)
    This is the second way to find \(b\text{,}\) and thus the line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=-33000x+878000\text{.}\)
  2. In the year \(2010\text{,}\) \(x\) was \(10\text{.}\) To find the amount remaining in the fund, we substitute \(10\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-33000x+878000\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -33000x+878000 \\ y \amp = -33000 \cdot 10 +878000 \\ y \amp = -330000+878000 \\ y \amp = 548000 \end{aligned} }\)
    So in the year \(2010\text{,}\) the fund had \({\$548{,}000}\) remaining in it.
  3. The fund will be empty when it has \({\$0}\) left in it. That is, \(y\) will equal \(0\text{.}\) To find how many years until this happens, we substitute \(0\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=-33000x+878000\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -33000x+878000 \\ 0 \amp = -33000x +878000 \\ 0\mathbf{{}-878000} \amp = -33000x +878000\mathbf{{}-878000} \\ -878000 \amp = -33000x \\ \frac{-878000}{-33000} \amp = \frac{-33000x}{-33000} \\ 26.61 \amp \approx x \end{aligned} }\)
    Approximately \(26.61\) years after 2000, in the year 2026, the fund will be depleted.
