Item 2.5.3.14.b.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] -x + t \amp = B \\ -x + t\mathbf{{} -t} \amp = B\mathbf{{} -t} \\ -x \amp = B-t \\ (-1) \cdot (-x) \amp =(-1) \cdot (B-t) \\ (-1) \cdot (-x) \amp =(-1) \cdot (B+(-t)) \\ x \amp =(-1) \cdot B + (-1) \cdot (-t) \\ x \amp = -B+t \quad (\text{already correct}) \\ x \amp =t-B \quad (\text{looks better}) \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({t-B}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({t-B}\) into the original equation for \(x\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
