Item 3.5.7.67.c.
To find when the tree was \(\) feet tall, we can substitute \(21.1\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.14x+14.1\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.14x+14.1 \\ 21.1 \amp = 0.14x +14.1 \\ 21.1\mathbf{{}-14.1} \amp = 0.14x +14.1\mathbf{{}-14.1}\\ 7 \amp = 0.14x \\ \frac{7}{0.14} \amp = \frac{0.14x}{0.14} \\ 50 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
So the tree was \(21.1\) feet tall after \(50\) months.
