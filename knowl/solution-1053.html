Explanation A.4.3.48.1.

This problem can be boiled down to this question: \(70.28\) is \(25.1\%\) of what? We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(70.28\) is \(25.1\%\) of \(x\text{,}\) so “\(25.1\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(70.28\) out of \(x\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{25.1}{100} \amp = \frac{70.28}{x} \\ 25.1x \amp = 100 \cdot 70.28 \\ 25.1x \amp = 7028 \\ \frac{25.1x}{25.1} \amp = \frac{7028}{25.1} \\ x \amp = 280 \end{aligned} }\)
The alcohol solution weighs \({280\ {\rm g}}\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: \(70.28\) is \(25.1\%\) of what? Assume \(70.28\) is \(25.1\%\) of \(x\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} 70.28 \amp = 0.251 \cdot x \\ \frac{70.28}{0.251} \amp = \frac{0.251x}{0.251} \\ 280 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
The alcohol solution weighs \({280\ {\rm g}}\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “\(70.28\) is \(25.1\%\) of what,”
  • \(70.28\) is the percentage,
  • \(25.1\%\) is the rate,
  • “what” is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{base} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{rate}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{base} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{rate}} = \frac{70.28}{25.1\%} = \frac{70.28}{0.251} = 280 }\)
The alcohol solution weighs \({280\ {\rm g}}\text{.}\)
