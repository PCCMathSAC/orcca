Item 2.5.3.12.a.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] x - 1 \amp = 9 \\ x - 1\mathbf{{}+1} \amp = 9\mathbf{{}+1} \\ x \amp = 10 \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \(10\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(10\) into the original equation for \(x\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
