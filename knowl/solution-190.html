Explanation 2.5.3.3.1.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} -3y - 7 \amp = {-16} \\ -3y - 7 \mathbf{{}+7} \amp = {-16}\mathbf{{}+7} \\ -3y \amp = {-9} \\ \frac{-3y}{-3} \amp = \frac{{-9}}{-3} \\ y \amp = 3 \end{aligned} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(3\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(y\text{,}\) some report \(y = 3\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{3\}\text{,}\) or that \(y\in\left\{3\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(3\) in for \(y\) in the original equation \(-3y-7=-16\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
