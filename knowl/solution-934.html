Explanation A.2.7.93.1.

We can address this problem as repeatedly taking away \({{\frac{5}{12}}}\) kilograms from \(25\) kilograms, which implies a division problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] 25 \div {{\frac{5}{12}}} \amp = \frac{25}{1} \cdot \frac{12}{5} \\ \amp = \frac{5}{1} \cdot \frac{12}{1} \\ \amp = \frac{5\cdot12}{1 \cdot 1} \\ \amp = {60} \end{aligned} }\)
\({60}\) families can be served with the \(25\) kilograms of food.
