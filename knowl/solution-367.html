Explanation 3.4.5.40.1.
One approach to this problem is to know that vertical lines do not have slope. So we would say that the slope Does Not Exist, and enter DNE.
To be more consistent with how you might approach problems like this that do not have verical lines, we would first need to identify two points that the line passes through. It is wise to choose points with integer coordinates. For this problem, we choose \((-3,0)\) and \((-3,1)\text{.}\)
Next, we sketch a slope triangle and find the rise and run. In the sketch below, the rise is \(1\) and the run is \(0\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned}\text{slope}\amp =\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}\\ \amp =\frac{1}{0}\\\amp =\text{DNE}\hbox{ or }\text{NONE} \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
This line’s slope is \(\text{DNE}\hbox{ or }\text{NONE}\text{.}\)
