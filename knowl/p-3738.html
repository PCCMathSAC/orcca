  1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] rt+A \amp = B \\ rt+A\mathbf{{} -rt} \amp = B \mathbf{{} -rt} \\ A \amp = {B-rt} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({B-rt}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({B-rt}\) into the original equation for \(A\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] rt+A \amp = B \\ rt+A\mathbf{{}-A} \amp = B \mathbf{{}-A} \\ rt \amp = B-A \\ \frac{rt}{t} \amp = \frac{B-A}{t} \\ r \amp = {\frac{B-A}{t}} \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({\frac{B-A}{t}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{B-A}{t}}\) into the original equation for \(r\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
