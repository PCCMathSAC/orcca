Item 2.5.3.20.a.
\(\begin{aligned} 4t +6 \amp = 34 \\ 4t +6\mathbf{{}-6} \amp = 34\mathbf{{}-6} \\ 4t \amp = 28 \\ \frac{4t}{4} \amp = \frac{28}{4} \\ t \amp = 7 \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \(7\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(7\) into the original equation for \(t\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
