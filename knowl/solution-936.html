Explanation A.2.7.95.1.

In this problem, each layer of tile is \({{\frac{2}{3}}}\) centimeters tall. The child will keep stacking up layers until the total height reaches \({10}\) centimeters. This is like asking how many “\({{\frac{2}{3}}}\) centimeters” are there in \({10}\) centimeters, a division problem.
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {10} \div {{\frac{2}{3}}} \amp = \frac{{10}}{1} \div \frac{2}{3} \\ \amp = \frac{{10}}{1} \cdot \frac{3}{2} \\ \amp = \frac{{5}}{1} \cdot \frac{3}{1} \\ \amp = \frac{{5} \cdot 3}{1 \cdot 1} \\ \amp = {15} \end{aligned} }\)
To reach the total height of \({10}\) centimeters, \({15}\) layers of tiles are needed.
