Item 3.6.5.66.c.
To find when the tree was \(24.12\) feet tall, we substitute \(24.12\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.23(x-11)+14.23\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.23(x-11) +14.23 \\ 24.12 \amp = 0.23(x-11) +14.23 \\ 9.89 \amp = 0.23(x-11)\\ \frac{9.89}{0.23} \amp = x - 11\\ 43 \amp = x - 11\\ 54 \amp = x\\ 54 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
So the tree was \(24.12\) feet tall after \(54\) months.
