Explanation A.4.3.69.1.

The house’s value two years ago was \({\$270{,}000}\text{.}\) After one year, the house’s value decreased by \(7\%\text{.}\)
The amount of decrease was \(7\%\) of \({\$270{,}000}\text{.}\) There are two methods to find this amount.
We can use proportion. Let \(x\) dollars be the amount of decrease, then we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{270000} \amp = \frac{7}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 270000\cdot7 \\ 100x \amp = 1890000 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{1890000}{100} \\ x \amp = 18900 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of decrease:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.07 \cdot 270000 = 18900 }\)
After one year, the house’s value became \({\$270{,}000}-{\$18{,}900}={\$251{,}100}\text{.}\)
After another year, the house’s value decreased by another \(7\%\text{.}\)
The amount of decrease was \(7\%\) of \({\$251{,}100}\text{.}\) There are two methods to find this amount.
We can use proportion. Let \(x\) dollars be the amount of decrease, then we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{251100} \amp = \frac{7}{100} \\ 100x \amp = 251100\cdot7 \\ 100x \amp = 1757700 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{1757700}{100} \\ x \amp = 17577 \end{aligned} }\)
Or we can use the percentage formula to find the amount of decrease:
\(\displaystyle{ 0.07 \cdot 251100 = 17577 }\)
After two years, the house’s value became \({\$251{,}100}-{\$17{,}577}={\$233{,}523}\text{.}\)
The house’s value this year is \({\$233{,}523}\text{.}\)
