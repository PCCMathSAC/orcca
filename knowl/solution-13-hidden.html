When \(d=5\text{,}\) we have:
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} h\amp= -2\abs{d-3}+6\\ h\amp= -2\abs{\substitute{5}-3}+6\amp\amp\text{Review order of operations in }\text{Section A.5}\text{.}\\ \amp= -2\abs{2}+6\amp\amp\text{Review absolute value in }\text{Section A.3}\text{.}\\ \amp= -2(2)+6\\ \amp= -4+6=2 \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
So when you are \(5\) ft from the west side, the height of the tent is \(2\) ft.
