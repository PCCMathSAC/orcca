Item 3.5.7.67.b.
After \(29\) months, we can find the height of the tree if we substitute \(29\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=0.14x+14.1\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.14x+14.1 \\ y \amp = 0.14 \cdot 29 +14.1 \\ y \amp = 4.06+14.1 \\ y \amp = 18.16 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(29\) months, the tree is \(\) feet tall.
