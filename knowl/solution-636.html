Explanation 3.10.9.25.1.

  1. A line’s equation in slope-intercept form looks like \(y=mx+b\text{,}\) where \(m\) is the slope, and \(y\) is the \(y\)-coordinate of the \(y\)-intercept. Since the gas is leaking at a rate of \(5.6\) grams per minute, we know that the slope of the linear model is \(-5.6\text{.}\)
    So now we have that \(y=-5.6x+b\text{.}\) The next step is to find the value of \(b\text{.}\) There are at least two ways.
    One way is to substitute a known point into \(y=-5.6x+b\text{.}\) We use the point \((7,240.8)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y \amp = -5.6x + b \\ 240.8 \amp = -5.6 \cdot 7 + b \\ 240.8 \amp = -39.2 + b \\ 240.8\mathbf{{} + 39.2} \amp = -39.2 + b\mathbf{{} + 39.2} \\ 280 \amp = b\\ b \amp = 280 \end{aligned}\)
    This line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=-5.6x+280\text{.}\)
    Another way is to use the line’s point-slope equation \(y-y_{1}=m(x-x_{1})\text{.}\) Again, we use the point \((7,240.8)\text{.}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} y-y_{1} \amp = m(x-x_{1}) \\ y-240.8 \amp = -5.6(x-7) \\ y-240.8 \amp = -5.6x - 5.6 \cdot (-7) \\ y-240.8 \amp = -5.6x+39.2 \\ y-240.8\mathbf{{}+240.8} \amp = -5.6x+39.2\mathbf{{}+240.8} \\ y \amp = -5.6x+280 \end{aligned}\)
    This is the second way to find \(b\text{,}\) and thus the line’s slope-intercept equation is \(y=-5.6x+280\text{.}\)
  2. After \(34\) minutes, we can find the mass of the remaining gas if we substitute \(34\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-5.6x+280\text{.}\) We have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -5.6x+280 \\ y \amp = -5.6 \cdot 34 +280 \\ y \amp = -190.4+280 \\ y \amp = 89.6 \end{aligned} }\)
    So after \(34\) minutes, there are \(89.6\) grams of gas remaining.
  3. To find when the gas will be all gone, we need to find when there are \(0\) grams of gas left. We can substitute \(0\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=-5.6x+280\text{,}\) and we have:
    \(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -5.6x+280 \\ 0 \amp = -5.6x +280 \\ 0\mathbf{{}-280} \amp = -5.6x +280\mathbf{{}-280}\\ -280 \amp = -5.6x \\ \frac{-280}{-5.6} \amp = \frac{-5.6x}{-5.6} \\ 50 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
    So after \(50\) minutes, the gas will be all gone.
