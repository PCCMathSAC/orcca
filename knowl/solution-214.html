Explanation 2.5.3.27.1.
Solution of Part a):
\(\begin{aligned}[t] r \amp = cm+x \\ r\mathbf{{}-x}\amp = cm +x\mathbf{{}-x} \\ r-x \amp = cm \\ \frac{r-x}{c} \amp = \frac{cm}{c} \\ {\frac{r-x}{c}} \amp = m\\ m \amp = {\frac{r-x}{c}} \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({\frac{r-x}{c}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{r-x}{c}}\) into the original equation for \(m\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
Solution of Part b):
\(\begin{aligned}[t] r \amp = cm +x \\ r\mathbf{{}-x}\amp = cm +x\mathbf{{}-x} \\ r-x \amp = cm \\ \frac{r-x}{m} \amp = \frac{cm}{m} \\ {\frac{r-x}{m}} \amp = c\\ c \amp = {\frac{r-x}{m}} \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({\frac{r-x}{m}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{r-x}{m}}\) into the original equation for \(c\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
