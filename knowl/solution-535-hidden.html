A line’s standard form is \(Ax+By=C\text{.}\) We need to move \(x\) and \(y\) terms to the same side of the equals sign.
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} {y}\amp={2x-1}\\ {y}\subtractright{2x}\amp={2x-1}\subtractright{2x}\\ {-2x+y}\amp=-1\\ (-1)({-2x+y})\amp=(-1)(-1)\\ {2x-y}\amp=1 \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
Note that \({-2x+y}=-1\) is already in standard form, but it’s better to get rid of the leading negative sign in an equation.
