Explanation A.4.3.59.1.

Out of \(3500\) registered voters, there are \(1365\) Democrats and \(1190\) Republicans. This implies there are \(3500-1365-1190=945\) Independents.
Now this problem can be boiled down to this question: \(945\) is what percent of \(3500\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(945\) is \(x\%\) of \(3500\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(945\) out of \(3500\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{945}{3500} \\ 3500x \amp = 945 \cdot 100 \\ 3500x \amp = 94500 \\ \frac{3500x}{3500} \amp = \frac{94500}{3500} \\ x \amp = 27 \end{aligned} }\)
In this town, \(27\%\) of all registered voters are Independents.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(945\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(3500\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 945 \amp = x \cdot 3500 \\ \frac{945}{3500} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 3500}{3500} \\ 0.27 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 27\% \end{aligned} }\)
In this town, \(27\%\) of all registered voters are Independents.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(945\) is what percent of \(3500\text{,}\)”
  • \(945\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(3500\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{945}{3500} = 0.27 = 27\% }\)
In this town, \(27\%\) of all registered voters are Independents.
