Out of \(4000\) registered voters, there are \(1400\) Democrats and \(920\) Republicans. This implies there are \(4000-1400-920=1680\) Independents.
Now this problem can be boiled down to this question: \(1680\) is what percent of \(4000\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(1680\) is \(x\%\) of \(4000\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(1680\) out of \(4000\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{1680}{4000} \\ 4000x \amp = 1680 \cdot 100 \\ 4000x \amp = 168000 \\ \frac{4000x}{4000} \amp = \frac{168000}{4000} \\ x \amp = 42 \end{aligned} }\)
In this town, \(42\%\) of all registered voters are Independents.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(1680\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(4000\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 1680 \amp = x \cdot 4000 \\ \frac{1680}{4000} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 4000}{4000} \\ 0.42 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 42\% \end{aligned} }\)
In this town, \(42\%\) of all registered voters are Independents.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(1680\) is what percent of \(4000\text{,}\)”
  • \(1680\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(4000\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{1680}{4000} = 0.42 = 42\% }\)
In this town, \(42\%\) of all registered voters are Independents.
