Item 1.5.6.72.b.
Consider which is larger: \(\frac{61}{44}\) or \(\frac{44}{61}\text{?}\) To make the largest value for \(x\text{,}\) you must put 61 in the left blank and 44 in the right blank because you solve this equation by dividing both sides by 44. This makes \(x = \frac{61}{44}\text{,}\) which is the largest possible value for \(x\text{.}\)
