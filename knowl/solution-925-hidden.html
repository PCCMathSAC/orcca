From a page’s total height, we subtract the thickness of the header and the height of the body to find the thickness of the footer:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {4 {\textstyle\frac{1}{6}}} - {{\frac{8}{9}}} - {3 {\textstyle\frac{2}{9}}} \amp = 4 + \frac{1}{6} -{{\frac{8}{9}}} - 3 - {{\frac{2}{9}}} \\ \amp = 1 + \frac{1}{6} -{{\frac{8}{9}}} - {{\frac{2}{9}}} \\ \amp = \frac{1}{1} + \frac{1}{6} -{{\frac{8}{9}}} - {{\frac{2}{9}}} \\ \amp = \frac{1 \cdot 18}{1 \cdot 18} + \frac{1\cdot3}{6\cdot3} - \frac{8\cdot2}{9\cdot2} - \frac{2\cdot2}{9\cdot2} \\ \amp = \frac{18}{18} + \frac{3}{18} - \frac{16}{18} - \frac{4}{18} \\ \amp = \frac{18+3-16-4}{18} \\ \amp = {{\frac{1}{18}}} \end{aligned} }\)
The footer is \({{\frac{1}{18}}}\) inches thick.
