To calculate the percentage increase/decrease, first we find the amount of increase/decrease by doing a simple subtraction calculation, and then we find the percentage increase/decrease.
In this problem, the amount of decrease is \(162-180=18\text{.}\)
Next, since we started with \(180\) cats, we need to ask: \(18\) is what percent of \(180\text{?}\)
Assume \(18\) is \(x\%\) of \(180\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(18\) out of \(180\)”.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{18}{180} \\ 180x \amp = 18 \cdot 100 \\ 180x \amp = 1800 \\ \frac{180x}{180} \amp = \frac{1800}{180} \\ x \amp = 10 \end{aligned} }\)
The percentage decrease of the shelter’s cat population is \(10\%\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Let \(x\) be the unknown percentage, and let the decrease \(18\) be \(x\) (percent) of \(180\text{.}\) That means:
\(\begin{aligned} 18 \amp = x \cdot 180 \\ \frac{18}{180} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 180}{180} \\ 0.1 \amp = x \\ 10\% \amp = x \\ \end{aligned}\)
The percentage decrease of the shelter’s cat population is \(10\%\text{.}\)
Method 3
We first divide the “new number” by the “original number”:
\(\displaystyle{ \frac{162}{180} = 0.9 = 90\% }\)
So the new number is \(90\%\) of the original number, implying the percentage decrease is \(100\% - 90\% = 10\%\text{.}\)
The percentage decrease of the shelter’s cat population is \(10\%\text{.}\)
