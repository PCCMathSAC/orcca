Explanation A.2.7.81.1.

This is a subtraction problem.
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned}[t] {{\frac{13}{42}}} - {{\frac{1}{7}}} \amp = \frac{13}{42}-\frac{1 \cdot 6}{7 \cdot 6} \\ \amp = \frac{13}{42} - \frac{6}{42} \\ \amp = \frac{13-6}{42} \\ \amp = \frac{7}{42} \\ \amp = {{\frac{1}{6}}} \end{aligned} }\)
The second leg is \({{\frac{1}{6}}}\) of a mile in length.
