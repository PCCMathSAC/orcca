1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] nx+B \amp = a \\ nx+B\mathbf{{} -nx} \amp = a \mathbf{{} -nx} \\ B \amp = {a-nx} \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({a-nx}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({a-nx}\) into the original equation for \(B\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] nx+B \amp = a \\ nx+B\mathbf{{}-B} \amp = a \mathbf{{}-B} \\ nx \amp = a-B \\ \frac{nx}{x} \amp = \frac{a-B}{x} \\ n \amp = {\frac{a-B}{x}} \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({\frac{a-B}{x}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{a-B}{x}}\) into the original equation for \(n\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
