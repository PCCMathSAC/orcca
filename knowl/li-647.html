Item 3.6.5.60.b.
In the year \(2011\text{,}\) \(x\) was \(11\text{.}\) To find the amount remaining in the fund, we substitute \(11\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-50000(x-4)+473000\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -50000(11-4) +473000 \\ y \amp = 123000 \end{aligned} }\)
So in the year \(2011\text{,}\) the fund had \({\$123{,}000}\) remaining in it.
