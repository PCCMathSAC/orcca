Explanation 2.5.3.14.1.
  1. \(\begin{aligned}[t] -y + 9 \amp = -1 \\ -y + 9\mathbf{{}-9} \amp = -1\mathbf{{}-9} \\ -y \amp = -10 \\ (-1) \cdot (-y) \amp = (-1) \cdot (-10) \\ y \amp = 10 \\ \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \(10\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(10\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
  2. \(\begin{aligned}[t] -x + t \amp = B \\ -x + t\mathbf{{} -t} \amp = B\mathbf{{} -t} \\ -x \amp = B-t \\ (-1) \cdot (-x) \amp =(-1) \cdot (B-t) \\ (-1) \cdot (-x) \amp =(-1) \cdot (B+(-t)) \\ x \amp =(-1) \cdot B + (-1) \cdot (-t) \\ x \amp = -B+t \quad (\text{already correct}) \\ x \amp =t-B \quad (\text{looks better}) \end{aligned}\)
    The solution to the equation is \({t-B}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({t-B}\) into the original equation for \(x\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
