Now that we’ve spent time making sure we understand the meaning of the numbers in this story, we can try to do what we were asked to do: write an equation where the solution represents how much time it will take to reach \(\$1700\text{.}\) It’s kind of a big deal to clearly identify what variable we will use. Our task is to write an equation where the solution represents “how much time…” and we are measuring time in months. So one perfectly fine choice we can make is to let \(m\) be our variable, where \(m\) stands for the number of months it will take to reach \(\$1700\text{.}\) (Another common choice would be to use \(t\text{,}\) since it stands for an amount of time.)
