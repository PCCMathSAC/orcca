Explanation 3.5.7.73.1.
  1. Since Line \(S\) has the equation \(y = ax + b\) and Line \(T\) has the equation \(y =cx + d\text{,}\) the variables \(a\) and \(c\) represent slopes. Since \(a \gt c \gt 0\text{,}\) we know that the slope of Line \(S\) is steeper than the slope of Line \(T\text{.}\) We also know that both slopes are positive.
  2. Since Line \(S\) has the equation \(y = ax + b\) and Line \(T\) has the equation \(y =cx + d\text{,}\) the variables \(b\) and \(b\) represent \(y\)-intercepts. Since \(b \gt d \gt 0\text{,}\) we know that Line \(S\) and Line \(T\) both intersect the \(y\)-axis at positive \(y\)-intercepts and that Line \(S\) intersects the \(y\)-axis at a higher point than Line \(T\text{.}\)
