\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} -5m + 5 \amp = -m -15 \\ -5m + 5\mathbf{{}+5m +15} \amp = -m -15\mathbf{{}+5m +15} \\ 20 \amp = 4m \\ \frac{20}{4} \amp = \frac{4m}{4} \\ 5 \amp = m\\ m \amp = 5 \end{aligned} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(5\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(m\text{,}\) some report \(m = 5\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{5\}\text{,}\) or that \(m\in\left\{5\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(5\) in for \(m\) in the original equation \(-5m+5=-m-15\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
