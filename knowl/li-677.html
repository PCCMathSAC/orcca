Item 3.6.5.65.b.
After \(39\) minutes, we can find the mass of the remaining gas if we substitute \(39\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-5(x-9)+180\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -5(39-9) +180 \\ y \amp = 30 \end{aligned} }\)
So after \(39\) minutes, there are \(30\) grams of gas remaining.
