Item 3.5.7.66.b.
In the year \(2011\text{,}\) \(x\) was \(11\text{.}\) To find the amount remaining in the fund, we substitute \(11\) in for \(x\) in the equation \(y=-23000x+896000\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -23000x+896000 \\ y \amp = -23000 \cdot 11 +896000 \\ y \amp = -253000+896000 \\ y \amp = 643000 \end{aligned} }\)
So in the year \(2011\text{,}\) the fund had \({\$643{,}000}\) remaining in it.
