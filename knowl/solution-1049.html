Explanation A.4.3.44.1.

We can use subtraction to find the percent of Sting signatures:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \amp \phantom{{}=}1-20\%-20\%-12\%-12\% \\ \amp =1-0.2-0.2-0.12-0.12 \\ \amp =0.36 \\ \amp = 36\% \end{aligned} }\)
So there are \(36\%\) Sting signatures. Now this problem can be boiled down to this question: What is \(36\%\) of \(1950\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(36\%\) of \(1950\) is \(x\text{,}\) so “\(36\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(x\) out of \(1950\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{36}{100} \amp = \frac{x}{1950} \\ 100x \amp = 36 \cdot 1950 \\ 100x \amp = 70200 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{70200}{100} \\ x \amp = 702 \end{aligned} }\)
There are \(702\) signatures by Sting in this collection.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: What is \(36\%\) of \(1950\text{?}\) Assume \(x\) is \(36\%\) of \(1950\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} x \amp = 0.36 \cdot 1950 \\ \amp = 702 \end{aligned} }\)
There are \(702\) signatures by Sting in this collection.
Method 3
In the sentence “What is \(36\%\) of \(1950\text{,}\)”
  • “what” is the percentage,
  • \(36\%\) is the rate,
  • \(1950\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{percentage} = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base}\text{,}\) we do a multiplication to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{percentage } = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base} = 36\% \cdot 1950 = 0.36 \cdot 1950 = 702 }\)
There are \(702\) signatures by Sting in this collection.
