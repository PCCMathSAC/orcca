Item 2.5.3.19.b.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] ay+p \amp = r \\ ay +p\mathbf{{}-p} \amp =r\mathbf{{}-p} \\ ay \amp = r-p \\ \frac{ay}{a} \amp = \frac{r-p}{a} \\ y \amp = {\frac{r-p}{a}} \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \({\frac{r-p}{a}}\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \({\frac{r-p}{a}}\) into the original equation for \(y\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
