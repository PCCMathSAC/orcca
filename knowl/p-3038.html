Since the container has been losing \(3\) oz of water every \(5\) minutes, it loses \(\frac{3}{5}\) oz every minute. At \(t\) minutes past 9:00 AM, the container will have lost \(\frac{3}{5}t\) oz of water. Since the intial amount was \(21\) oz, the amount of water in the container (in oz) can be modeled by \(21-\frac{3}{5}t\text{.}\)
