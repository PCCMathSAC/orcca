Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(80\%\) of \(880\) is \(x\text{,}\) so “\(80\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(x\) out of \(880\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{80}{100} \amp = \frac{x}{880} \\ 100x \amp = 80 \cdot 880 \\ 100x \amp = 70400 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{70400}{100} \\ x \amp = 704 \end{aligned} }\)
\(80\%\) of \(880\) is \(704\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: What is \(80\%\) of \(880\text{?}\) Assume \(x\) is \(80\%\) of \(880\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} x \amp = 0.8 \cdot 880 \\ \amp = 704 \end{aligned} }\)
\(80\%\) of \(880\) is \(704\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “\(80\%\) of \(880\) is what,”
  • “what” is the percentage,
  • \(80\%\) is the rate,
  • \(880\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{percentage} = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base}\text{,}\) we do a multiplication to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{percentage } = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base} = 80\% \cdot 880 = 0.8 \cdot 880 = 704 }\)
\(80\%\) of \(880\) is \(704\text{.}\)
