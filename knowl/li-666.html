Item 3.6.5.63.c.
The fund will be empty when it has \({\$0}\) left in it. That is, \(y\) will equal \(0\text{.}\) To find how many years until this happens, we substitute \(0\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=-29000(x-4)+540000\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = -29000(x-4) +540000 \\ 0 \amp = -29000(x-4) +540000 \\ -540000 \amp = -29000(x-4)\\ \frac{-540000}{-29000} \amp = x - 4\\ 18.6206896551724 \amp = x - 4\\ 22.6206896551724 \amp = x\\ 22.62 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
Approximately \(22.62\) years after 2000, in the year 2022, the fund will be depleted.
