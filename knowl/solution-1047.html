Explanation A.4.3.42.1.

The price changed from \({\$700.00}\) to \({\$490.00}\text{,}\) implying the markdown was \({\$700.00} - {\$490.00} = {\$210.00}\text{.}\)
Now this problem can be boiled down to this question: \(210\) is what percent of \(700\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(210\) is \(x\%\) of \(700\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(210\) out of \(700\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{210}{700} \\ 700x \amp = 210 \cdot 100 \\ 700x \amp = 21000 \\ \frac{700x}{700} \amp = \frac{21000}{700} \\ x \amp = 30 \end{aligned} }\)
The painting was \(30\%\) off its original price.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(210\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(700\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 210 \amp = x \cdot 700 \\ \frac{210}{700} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 700}{700} \\ 0.3 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 30\% \end{aligned} }\)
The painting was \(30\%\) off its original price.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(210\) is what percent of \(700\text{,}\)”
  • \(210\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(700\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{210}{700} = 0.3 = 30\% }\)
The painting was \(30\%\) off its original price.
