Explanation A.4.3.41.1.

The price changed from \({\$650.00}\) to \({\$585.00}\text{,}\) implying the markdown was \({\$650.00} - {\$585.00} = {\$65.00}\text{.}\)
Now this problem can be boiled down to this question: \(65\) is what percent of \(650\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem.
Assume \(65\) is \(x\%\) of \(650\text{,}\) so “\(x\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(65\) out of \(650\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{x}{100} \amp = \frac{65}{650} \\ 650x \amp = 65 \cdot 100 \\ 650x \amp = 6500 \\ \frac{650x}{650} \amp = \frac{6500}{650} \\ x \amp = 10 \end{aligned} }\)
The painting was \(10\%\) off its original price.
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem.
This translation from English to may should help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \Longleftrightarrow 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
Assume \(65\) is \(x\) (as a percent) of \(650\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} 65 \amp = x \cdot 650 \\ \frac{65}{650} \amp = \frac{x \cdot 650}{650} \\ 0.1 \amp = x\\ x \amp = 10\% \end{aligned} }\)
The painting was \(10\%\) off its original price.
Method 3
In the sentence “\(65\) is what percent of \(650\text{,}\)”
  • \(65\) is the percentage,
  • “what percent” is the rate,
  • \(650\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}}\text{,}\) we do a division to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{rate} = \frac{\text{percentage}}{\text{base}} = \frac{65}{650} = 0.1 = 10\% }\)
The painting was \(10\%\) off its original price.
