Consider which is greater: \(61 - 44\) or \(44 - 61\text{?}\) To make the largest value for \(x\text{,}\) you must put 44 in the left blank and 61 in the right blank because you solve this equation by subtracting 44 from both sides. This makes \(x = 61 - 44\text{,}\) which is the largest possible value for \(x\text{.}\)
