The revenue consists of \(3\) parts. The total revenue is like a big \(1\text{;}\) ticket sales account for \({{\frac{1}{7}}}\text{;}\) auction sales account for \({{\frac{1}{2}}}\text{.}\) To find the missing piece (from donations), we use subtraction.
Note that a common denominator of \(1\text{,}\) \({{\frac{1}{7}}}\) and \({{\frac{1}{2}}}\) is \(14\text{.}\)
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] 1 - {{\frac{1}{7}}} - {{\frac{1}{2}}} \amp = \frac{14}{14} - \frac{1\cdot2}{7\cdot2} - \frac{1\cdot7}{2\cdot7} \\ \amp = \frac{14-2-7}{14} \\ \amp = {{\frac{5}{14}}} \end{aligned} }\)
So \({{\frac{5}{14}}}\) of the revenue came from donations.
