Item 2.5.3.13.a.
\(\begin{aligned}[t] -x + 9 \amp = 5 \\ -x + 9\mathbf{{}-9} \amp = 5\mathbf{{}-9} \\ -x \amp = -4 \\ (-1) \cdot (-x) \amp = (-1) \cdot (-4) \\ x \amp = 4 \\ \end{aligned}\)
The solution to the equation is \(4\text{;}\) we can check our work by substituting \(4\) into the original equation for \(x\text{;}\) this is left as an exercise.
