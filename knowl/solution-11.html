Explanation 1.1.8.1.
This model uses \(x\) as the number of years since 2000. So for the year 2030, \(x\) is \(30\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} 620+32.35(\substitute{30})\amp = 1590.50 \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
According to this model, the median monthly rent for a living unit in Oregon in 2030 will be \(\$1590.50\text{.}\)
