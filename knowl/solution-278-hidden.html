This solution will use the \(x\) values \(-1\text{,}\) \(0\text{,}\) \(1\text{,}\) \(2\) and \(3\text{.}\) The choice to use these \(x\)-values is arbitrary. Since they are small numbers, they might make calculations easier.
One at a time, we substitute these \(x\)-values into the equation \(y=-10x-4\text{,}\) and solve for \(y\)
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} y = -10\cdot \left(-1\right)-4 \amp \implies y=6\\ y = -10\cdot 0-4 \amp \implies y=-4\\ y = -10\cdot 1-4 \amp \implies y=-14\\ y = -10\cdot 2-4 \amp \implies y=-24\\ y = -10\cdot 3-4 \amp \implies y=-34\\ \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
