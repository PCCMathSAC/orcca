Item 3.5.7.72.c.
To find when the tree was \(30.76\) feet tall, we can substitute \(30.76\) in for \(y\) in the equation \(y=0.26x+16.2\text{,}\) and we have:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned} y \amp = 0.26x+16.2 \\ 30.76 \amp = 0.26x +16.2 \\ 30.76\mathbf{{}-16.2} \amp = 0.26x +16.2\mathbf{{}-16.2}\\ 14.56 \amp = 0.26x \\ \frac{14.56}{0.26} \amp = \frac{0.26x}{0.26} \\ 56 \amp = x \end{aligned} }\)
So the tree was \(30.76\) feet tall after \(56\) months.
