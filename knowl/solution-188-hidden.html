\(\displaystyle{ \begin{alignedat}{2} 6p + 2 \amp = {56} \\ 6p + 2\mathbf{{}-2}\amp = {56} \mathbf{{}-2} \\ 6p \amp = {54} \\ \frac{6p}{6} \amp = \frac{{54}}{6} \\ p \amp = 9 \end{alignedat} }\)
The solution to this equation is \(9\text{.}\) To stress that this is a value assigned to \(p\text{,}\) some report \(p = 9\text{.}\) We can also say that the solution set is \(\{9\}\text{,}\) or that \(p\in\left\{9\right\}\text{.}\) If we substitute \(9\) in for \(p\) in the original equation \(6p+2=56\text{,}\) the equation will be true. Please check this on your own; it is an important habit.
