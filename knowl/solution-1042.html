Explanation A.4.3.37.1.

First, we need to find the amount of increase in price. It’s given that the watch’s price was marked up by \(30\%\) of its original price, \({\$260.00}\text{.}\)
The problem can be boiled down to this question: What is \(30\%\) of \(260\text{?}\) We will show multiple methods to solve this problem.
Method 1
We will use proportion to solve this problem. Assume \(30\%\) of \(260\) is \(x\text{,}\) so “\(30\) out of \(100\)” corresponds to “\(x\) out of \(260\)”.
We will write and solve the proportion:
\(\displaystyle{\begin{aligned}[t] \frac{30}{100} \amp = \frac{x}{260} \\ 100x \amp = 30 \cdot 260 \\ 100x \amp = 7800 \\ \frac{100x}{100} \amp = \frac{7800}{100} \\ x \amp = 78 \end{aligned} }\)
The amount of price increase was \({\$78.00}\text{,}\) so the new price is \({\$260.00}+{\$78.00}={\$338.00}\text{.}\)
So the watch’s markup price is \({\$338.00}\text{.}\)
Method 2
We will use the percentage formula to solve this problem. This translation from English to math may help you remember the percentage formula.
\(2 \text{ is } 50\% \text{ of } 4 \iff 2 = 0.5 \cdot 4\)
The question is: What is \(30\%\) of \(260\text{?}\) Assume \(x\) is \(30\%\) of \(260\text{.}\) We have:
\(\displaystyle{ \begin{aligned} x \amp = 0.3 \cdot 260 \\ \amp = 78 \end{aligned} }\)
The amount of price increase was \({\$78.00}\text{,}\) so the new price is \({\$260.00}+{\$78.00}={\$338.00}\text{.}\)
So the watch’s markup price is \({\$338.00}\text{.}\)
Method 3
In the sentence “What is \(30\%\) of \(260\text{,}\)”
  • “what” is the percentage,
  • \(30\%\) is the rate,
  • \(260\) is the base (following the word “of”).
By the formula \(\text{percentage} = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base}\text{,}\) we do a multiplication to solve the problem:
\(\displaystyle{ \text{percentage } = \text{rate} \cdot \text{base} = 30\% \cdot 260 = 0.3 \cdot 260 = 78 }\)
The amount of price increase was \({\$78.00}\text{,}\) so the new price is \({\$260.00}+{\$78.00}={\$338.00}\text{.}\)
So the watch’s markup price is \({\$338.00}\text{.}\)
