With the expression \(24\div(15\div 3+1) +2\text{,}\) we’ll simplify what’s inside the parentheses according to the order of operations, and then take \(24\) divided by that expression as our last step:
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} 24\div(15\div 3+1) +2\amp= 24\div(\nextoperation{15\div 3}+1) +2\\ \amp=24\div(\nextoperation{\highlight{5}+1}) +2\\ \amp=\nextoperation{24\div \highlight{6}}+2\\ \amp=\highlight{4}+2\\ \amp=\highlight{6} \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
